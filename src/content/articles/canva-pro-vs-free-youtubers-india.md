## Free plan: YouTuber ke liye asal me kya kaafi hai?

Free plan ko halke me mat lijiye. Ek naye YouTuber ke liye isme kaafi kuch hai:

- **YouTube templates ready hain:** Thumbnail (1280×720), channel art (2560×1440), Shorts (9:16 vertical) aur community post — sabke sizes aur templates free me milte hain. Size galat hone ka risk nahi.
- **1.6M+ templates aur 4.7M+ free assets:** Text-based thumbnails, simple layouts aur free stock photos ke liye ye library kaafi badi hai.
- **5 GB storage:** Shuruat me kaafi hai — 100–200 designs aaram se aate hain.
- **1 Brand Kit (3 colours):** Channel ke 3 main colours save kar sakte hain, fonts manual set karne padte hain.

Kami tab mehsoos hoti hai jab channel badhne lagta hai. Premium templates par crown icon dikhta hai — unhe use karne par ya to watermark aata hai ya paywall khulta hai. Aapki photo ko background se kaatkar thumbnail me lagana ho to Free me ye tool hi nahi hai — alag se background-removal site par jana padta hai. Aur ek hi design ko thumbnail se Shorts aur community post ke size me badalna manual kaam hai: har size ke liye design dobara banana padta hai.

Ek aur practical limit: 5 GB storage. Har video ke 2–3 design versions rakhne wale creator ke liye ye 6–8 mahine me bhar sakta hai. Tab purane designs delete karne padte hain — ya upgrade.

## Pro ke 5 features jo YouTuber ke kaam aate hain

Pro ki keemat ₹4,000/year hai. Sawal ye hai ki ye paise kis cheez ke hain. YouTuber ke workflow me paanch features asal farak banate hain:

### 1. Background Remover — thumbnail cutout 10 second me

Ye Pro ka sabse zyada use hone wala tool hai. Apni photo upload karein, ek click me background hat jata hai, aur cutout ko thumbnail par kahin bhi laga sakte hain. Free me ye kaam alag tools par karna padta hai — har thumbnail par 5–10 minute extra. Hafte me 3 thumbnails banane wale creator ke liye ye mahine me 2–3 ghante bachata hai.

### 2. Magic Resize — ek design, har format

Ek thumbnail banaya, ab usi ko Shorts (9:16), community post (square) aur channel banner me chahiye? Magic Resize ek click me design ko har size me badal deta hai, layout khud adjust ho jata hai. Bina iske har format ke liye design dobara banana padta hai — ya size galat hone par YouTube khud crop kar deta hai.

### 3. Brand Kit — channel ki pehchan consistent

Pro me 5 Brand Kits milte hain — har kit me colours, fonts aur logo save hote hain. Matlab har thumbnail me wahi fonts, wahi colour scheme, bina har baar dhoondhe. Free me sirf ek kit aur sirf 3 colours milte hain. Consistent thumbnails se viewers aapke videos ko feed me pehchanne lagte hain — ye CTR par seedha asar dalta hai.

### 4. Premium stock (141M+ assets) — crown icon se azadi

Free plan me achhe stock photos aur videos par crown icon laga hota hai. Pro me 141M+ premium photos, videos, graphics aur audio khul jate hain. Thumbnail ke background ke liye achhi stock photo ya Shorts ke liye B-roll clip chahiye ho to ye library kaam aati hai — alag se stock site ka subscription lene ki zaroorat nahi padti.

### 5. Social Content Planner + 100 GB storage

Pro me social posts schedule kar sakte hain — community posts aur Shorts ke promos pehle se plan karke. Aur 100 GB storage ka matlab hai saalon ke designs, brand assets aur video thumbnails bina delete kiye rakhna.

In paanch ke alawa Magic Edit/Eraser, translate aur bulk create bhi Pro me hain — lekin ek akele YouTuber ke liye upar wale paanch hi faisla karte hain.

## YouTuber ka Pro workflow: ek thumbnail kaise banta hai
