## Red flags: in cheezon se bacho

- **"Expected" dates ko pakka mat mano.** Amazon ki 1–8 November wave abhi bhi sirf tracker-reported hai — Amazon ne iski koi announcement nahi ki hai. Flipkart ki window isse alag hai: uski 10 November tak ki date Flipkart ki apni offer terms se confirmed hai (neeche update note dekho).
- **"Up to 80–90% off" MRP ka khel hai.** Asli deal wahi hai jahan current price pichhle 30–60 din ki price history se clearly neeche ho. Price tracker ya Amazon ka price-history tool use karo.
- **Exchange value ka "up to" trap.** Advertised exchange value best-condition device ki hai. Aapke phone ki actual value checkout par quote hogi aur pickup-time condition check ke baad final hogi. Pehle data backup + factory reset kar lo.
- **Cap khatm hone ka dhokha.** Bank offer ka cap per-card hota hai — ek card se cap khatm ho to doosre eligible card se khareedo, ya agle cap-refresh window ka wait karo (jaise GIF Reloaded me 24 October ko hua tha).
- **Fake urgency.** "Sale khatm hone wali hai" wale countdown aksar wave badalne par reset ho jaate hain. Price aur bank offer dekho, countdown dekhkar panic me mat khareedo.
