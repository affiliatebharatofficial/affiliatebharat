## Free domain ka sach: "free" sirf 1 saal ke liye hai

Pehle ye clear kar lete hain, kyunki yahi sabse bada misunderstanding hai:

- **Free domain = pehle saal ka registration fee ₹0.** Domain aapke naam par register hota hai, aapke hosting account me manage hota hai.
- **Dusre saal se renewal aapko dena hai** — standard registrar rate par (₹800–1,600/saal, extension par depend karta hai).
- **60-day transfer lock:** ICANN rule ke hisaab se naya ya transfer hua domain pehle 60 din kisi aur registrar par move nahi ho sakta. Matlab renewal ke time saste registrar par jaane ka plan hai to 60 din pehle se sochna hoga.
- **TLD restrictions:** Free domain har extension par nahi milta. Hostinger par qualifying TLDs hain .com, .net, .org, .in (third-party sources se; official page par exact list publish nahi hai). MilesWeb ke case me official page par extension list nahi hai — review sites .com/.in/.co.in batate hain.

## Teesra rasta: domain alag, hosting alag

Combo sabke liye best nahi hai. Agar aap thoda technical ho to ye split strategy aksar sasti padti hai:

1. **Domain Porkbun ya Cloudflare se lo** — .com ka renewal Porkbun par flat ~$11 (~₹920, hamesha), Cloudflare par wholesale rate. GoDaddy ka .com renewal ~₹1,599/saal hai — usse aadha. (Detail me humne [domain renewal guide](/domain-renewal-coupon-india-godaddy-alternative/) me cover kiya hai.)
2. **Hosting jahan sasti mile lo** — MilesWeb Starter ya Hostinger Single.
3. Domain ke DNS me hosting ke nameserver daal do — 10 minute ka kaam hai, har host ki knowledge base me steps hain.

Trade-off: setup me 10-15 minute extra lagte hain aur do dashboards manage karne padte hain. Fayda: dusre saal se domain renewal hamesha sasta, aur host badalna ho to domain already aapke control me hai.

## Combo lete time 5-point checklist

1. **Free domain kaun se plan par hai** — Hostinger me Single bahar hai; MilesWeb me aaj Starter par bhi hai (verify karte raho, plans badalte hain).
2. **Renewal price card par likhi hai ya nahi** — likhi hai to budget pakka; nahi hai to buffer rakho.
3. **GST jodkar dekho** — dono hosts "prices exclude 18% GST" likhte hain; ₹7,873 wala Premium checkout par hi pata chalta hai.
4. **Term length vs refund** — 36/48 mahine ka upfront bada hota hai; dono par 30-day money-back hai (domain fee refundable nahi — ye industry standard hai).
5. **60-day lock yaad rakho** — free domain pehle 60 din transfer nahi hoga; renewal planning usi hisaab se karo.
