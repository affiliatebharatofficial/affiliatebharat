Running a small business means customer records start out in an Excel sheet or WhatsApp chats, and that works — until leads cross 100 and follow-ups start slipping. Who was supposed to be called when, which quotation is still unanswered, which deal's payment is due. That is the point where a CRM (customer relationship management) tool earns its keep. And the good news is that you do not need to spend anything to start: several big CRM tools have free plans that are genuinely free — forever, with no credit card.

But "free" does not mean the same thing on every tool. One caps you at 2 users, another at 3; one allows 500 records, another 5,000. And one tool still appears in old guides with a free plan that no longer exists on its official page. This article compares five popular CRMs — **HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Zoho Bigin, Bitrix24 and Freshsales** — with prices and limits verified live on 20 November 2026. No tool was bought or tested; this comparison is based on an analysis of public specs, official pricing pages and independent reviews.

| CRM | Free users | Contacts / records | What stands out on free | First paid plan |

| **Zoho CRM Free** | 3 | 5,000 records | 5 workflow rules, Hindi interface | Standard ₹800/user/mo (annual) + GST |

## HubSpot Free CRM: the most complete free plan, up to 2 people

HubSpot's free CRM is the biggest name on this list — and its greatest strength is that the free plan covers more than just storing contacts. It covers a whole small sales cycle: contact management, deal pipeline, email tracking, meeting scheduling, live chat, forms and a reporting dashboard. The official page still says it plainly: "100% free with no expiration date" and "No credit card required."

![HubSpot's free CRM page on 15 November 2026 — "Free CRM Software for Startups & Small Businesses", "100% free with no expiration date", "No credit card required"](/screenshots/hubspot-free-crm-2026.png)
*HubSpot's free CRM page, captured 15 November 2026 — the free plan has no expiry and asks for no credit card; the wording was re-verified live on 20 November 2026*

But the real limits of the free plan are on the pricing page, not the homepage — and they were confirmed again today:

- **Up to 2 users** and **1,000 contacts** (for accounts created after September 2024)
- **1,000 records across all other standard object types** per account — a limit the FAQ now states explicitly
- **2,000 marketing emails a month**, with HubSpot branding
- **3 email templates** and **1 meetings link**
- Only **1 automated action** — so even basic automation like a welcome email is limited

One correction from the earlier Hindi version of this article: it said the free plan includes 1 deal pipeline. That figure came from independent readings of HubSpot's pricing pages in October–November 2026, but on 20 November 2026 the Free cell in both the Sales Hub and CRM comparison tables is blank — no pipeline number is stated for Free at all. Starter is "Up to 15 total pipelines", Professional 100, Enterprise 350. So do not plan around a stated free pipeline count; check the comparison table yourself before signing up.

Older guides describe HubSpot's free plan as "unlimited contacts" — that was true before September 2024, when the limit was 10 lakh contacts. Today it is wrong, so do not try to import 5,000 contacts on the strength of it — you will hit a wall at the 1,001st contact.

**The paid step:** Sales Hub Starter is listed at **$15/seat/month on annual billing** ($20 on monthly billing) on the USD price list. But Indian visitors today see something different: **₹585/seat/month on annual billing** (list price ₹1,670 struck through, "Save up to 65%") and **₹835/seat/month on monthly billing**. Professional is ₹7,497 a month on annual billing (₹8,330 monthly) and Enterprise ₹12,500 — against USD list prices of $50 and $75. The INR figures are new-customer discounts, so treat the checkout price as final. One more cost to budget for: the page footnotes show one-time onboarding fees — Professional ₹125,000 and Enterprise ₹292,000. For Indian users there is another wrinkle: HubSpot's prices are in dollars on the list, and whether Indian checkout shows INR or USD was not verified today — keep the card's forex charge in mind.

**Who it is for:** a 1–2 person team with under 1,000 contacts that also wants email marketing free. Freelancers, consultants and small agencies will find this the safest place to start.

## Zoho CRM Free: best for a 3-person team, and it speaks Hindi

Zoho CRM is the most-used CRM in India — and the reason is not just price, it is India-first design: INR billing, GST invoices, Indian phone formats and a Hindi interface. Zoho's pricing-page FAQ, read live on 20 November 2026, says the free edition supports **up to 3 users** and that Zoho will "never force you to move to a paid plan."

![Zoho CRM's pricing page on 15 November 2026 — annual billing: Standard ₹800, Professional ₹1,400, Enterprise ₹2,400 (Most Popular) and Ultimate ₹2,600 per user/month](/screenshots/zoho-crm-pricing-2026.png)
*Zoho CRM's pricing page, captured 15 November 2026 — Indian visitors see rupee prices on annual billing; the same figures were re-verified live on 20 November 2026*

What the free edition includes (from Zoho's feature comparison table, checked today):

- **3 users**, free forever, no credit card
- **5,000 records** (10 MB data storage) — contacts, deals and tasks combined
- **5 active workflow rules** — follow-up reminders and lead assignment automation free; HubSpot's free plan does not offer this
- **50 individual emails per user per day** (maximum 150 per organisation), **10 email templates**, **1 GB file storage**
- **5,000 API calls a day** — for connecting your own tools
- Only the **default pipeline** — extra pipelines are on paid plans
- Mass email is **not included** on the free plan

Zoho's FAQ also confirms that the CRM supports **28 languages, including Hindi**. That matters for a small Indian business: the team member who is not comfortable in English can still work in a Hindi interface.

**The paid step:** India prices were confirmed in today's live render — Standard **₹800/user/month**, Professional **₹1,400**, Enterprise **₹2,400** and Ultimate **₹2,600** on annual billing, GST extra. Monthly billing is higher: ₹1,300 / ₹2,100 / ₹3,000 / ₹3,200. US prices are $14/$23/$40/$52. For 3 users on Standard, the bill is ₹800 × 3 = ₹2,400/month, and adding 18% GST makes it **₹2,832/month**; over a year that is ₹28,800 + ₹5,184 GST = **₹33,984**. A GST-registered business can claim the GST back as input tax credit, so the effective cost stays around ₹2,400/month.

**Who it is for:** a 3-person team that wants automation free as well, and needs INR billing plus a GST invoice. Coaching institutes, real estate agents and small B2B teams will find this the default choice.

## Zoho Bigin: the simplest CRM for a solo founder

Bigin is Zoho's smaller sibling — not a full CRM, but a pipeline-first tool built for people who currently track enquiries in Excel or WhatsApp. Setup takes about half an hour: one board, deals on it, move the stages along.

The free plan is very clear — **1 user, 500 records, 1 pipeline and 3 automations** — and these figures matched across multiple independent readings in October–November 2026. After the 15-day trial ends, the account drops to the Free plan on its own; nothing is charged and no card is asked for. One practical note: Bigin's pricing page lives at **bigin.com/pricing.html** — opening /pricing/ shows Bigin's own "Page Not Found", confirmed in a live check.

*Bigin's pricing page, captured 15 November 2026 — rupee prices on yearly billing; the Free card clearly shows "Single Pipeline, 500 Records"; the same figures were re-verified live on 20 November 2026*

**The paid step:** Express **₹400/user/month**, Premier **₹720** and Bigin 360 **₹1,080** on annual billing, GST extra — confirmed in today's live render. Monthly billing is ₹550 / ₹900 / ₹1,260. US prices are $7/$12/$18. One user on Express bills at ₹400 + 18% GST = **₹472/month**.

**Who it is for:** a solo founder, freelancer or single salesperson who only needs to track deals and has no time to learn a full CRM. The moment a second member joins, Bigin Free stops working — then look at Zoho CRM Free (3 users).

## Bitrix24 Free: the wildcard for 5+ person teams — but there is a conflict

Bitrix24 is the most different tool on this list: not just a CRM, but tasks, team chat, video calls and file storage in one place. Its billing is different too — a **flat rate**, not per-user: the whole organisation pays one fixed price, whether there are 5 users or 50. That is why the free plan looks attractive for a larger team.

But there is a conflict here that would be dishonest to hide. Bitrix24's official FAQ (read live today) says the free plan lets you add "an unlimited number of users", with no time limit. **However**, the Free card on Bitrix24's India pricing page (bitrix24.in/prices — rendered live today) clearly says **"for 1–2 users"**. Multiple independent readings of the vendor page in October 2026 also found "1 to 2 users". This article treats the Free card's 1–2 user limit as the operative one, because that is what you will see when you sign up — but keep the FAQ claim in mind, and read the Free card's line yourself before signing up.

![Bitrix24's India pricing page on 20 November 2026 — Free "100% free" (1–2 users), Basic ₹1,910, Standard ₹4,790, Professional ₹9,590 per organisation/month, billed annually](/screenshots/bitrix24-pricing-2026-en.png)
*Bitrix24's India pricing page on 20 November 2026 — flat pricing, not per-user; the Free card clearly says "for 1–2 users"*

The rest of the free plan's limits (from a 5 October 2026 reading of the plan comparison table): **5 GB storage**, **1,000 contacts and 1,000 deals**, **1 pipeline**, **5 sales automation rules** — and telephony and Market apps are only on paid plans. One third-party report mentions accounts being deleted after 50 days of inactivity — that was not confirmed on the official page, so treat it as reported, but logging in regularly is still the safe habit. Note also that today's page states no numeric contacts/deals limit at all, so the 1,000 figure is carried as a reported reading, not a live page fact.

**The paid step:** Basic **₹1,910/organisation/month** on annual billing (5 users included) — the India price; the US page lists Basic at $49/month, so regional pricing in India is much cheaper. Standard is ₹4,790 (50 users) and Professional ₹9,590 (100 users). Enterprise, new in today's check, is ₹19,190 (250 users by default, 3 TB storage). Flat pricing means a 5-user team pays ₹1,910/month for the whole team on Basic — ₹382/user/month, half of Zoho Standard (₹800). But the 6th user forces a jump to Standard (₹4,790) — there is no plan in between.

**Who it is for:** a team that wants CRM plus tasks and chat in one place, and prefers flat billing. But verify the free plan's user cap yourself before relying on it.

Signing up gives a 21-day full-featured trial, and the FAQ is explicit: when the trial ends you select a plan and pay, and "If you choose not to continue, your account will be suspended."

## The India questions: INR, GST, UPI, Hindi and WhatsApp

**INR billing:** Zoho CRM, Bigin and Bitrix24 show rupee prices on their India pages — confirmed in today's live renders. HubSpot and Freshsales pages show dollars on the list, though both flip to INR for Indian visitors (HubSpot ₹585/seat, Freshsales ₹749/user); what Indian checkout actually shows was not verified today.

**GST:** Zoho and Bigin prices are before GST; 18% GST is added on the invoice. A GST-registered business can claim input tax credit. Bitrix24's page also says local taxes (VAT/GST) apply depending on locality.

**UPI payment:** Zoho's FAQ lists Visa, MasterCard, American Express, PayPal and bank transfer as payment methods — **UPI is not mentioned**. HubSpot and Bitrix24 pages do not list UPI either. So arrange an international card or PayPal before buying a paid plan.

**Hindi interface:** Zoho CRM supports 28 languages, including Hindi — confirmed from the official FAQ. No clear claim about Hindi support was found on the other tools' official pages, so none is made here.

**WhatsApp:** WhatsApp Business integration in Bigin starts with the Express plan (₹400/user/month). In Zoho CRM, WhatsApp integration comes through paid plans and separate add-ons — do not expect it on the free plan. If your sales happen on WhatsApp, this can be a deciding factor.

## Which CRM for whom — the direct answer

- **Solo founder / freelancer, only need to track deals:** Bigin Free. Simple, fast, 500 records is enough.
- **2 people, also want email marketing free:** HubSpot Free. 1,000 contacts and 2,000 emails a month — no other free plan on this list matches that combination.
- **A 3-person small team that wants automation:** Zoho CRM Free. 5,000 records and 5 workflow rules free — and the Hindi interface is a bonus.
- **A 5+ person team, ₹0 budget, CRM + tasks + chat in one place:** Bitrix24 Free — but read the Free card's "1–2 users" line yourself first, because the FAQ says something else.
- **Most calls happen on the phone and you need built-in calling:** look at Freshsales Growth — but it is not free, it starts at $9/user/month.

## How to stay on a free plan longer

1. **Count your contacts before importing.** HubSpot's 1,000 and Zoho's 5,000 record limits run out first. Clean out old, dead leads first — every dead record eats free limit.
2. **Start with one pipeline.** Every free plan here gives you a single pipeline (where stated). Trying to build separate pipelines for separate products will force a paid plan sooner.
3. **Budget your automation.** Zoho gives 5 workflow rules free — spend them on the most repeated job (like a follow-up reminder on a new lead). HubSpot's free plan has almost no automation, so build a manual follow-up system there from day one.
4. **Keep an export backup.** Free plan or paid, export your contacts as a CSV once a month. In a Freshsales-type situation — trial over, account suspended — getting your data out can become difficult.
5. **Write down your upgrade trigger in advance.** For example: "when contacts cross 800, we will budget for HubSpot Starter" — that way you will not buy the wrong plan in a panic midway.
