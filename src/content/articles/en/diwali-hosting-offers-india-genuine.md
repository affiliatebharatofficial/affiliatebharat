## The real Diwali math: renewal + GST + domain

The biggest trap in festive hosting banners is always the renewal price. One worked example:

- **Hostinger Premium:** ₹149/mo × 48 months = ₹7,152 for the first term. Renewal at ₹449/mo means the same plan costs about ₹21,552 for the next 48 months — **3x the first cycle**.
- **MilesWeb Premium:** ₹129/mo × 36 months = ₹4,644 for the first term. Renewal at ₹289/mo (their own table) means the next 36 months cost about ₹10,404 — **2.24x the first cycle**.

Then add **18% GST** on top of everything: a ₹149/mo plan becomes ~₹176/mo at checkout, a ₹289/mo renewal becomes ~₹341/mo. And the free domain lasts only year one — from year two, a .com renewal runs roughly ₹800–1,200/year on its own.

**The genuine-deal formula:** (intro price × term + GST) + (renewal price × expected years + GST) + domain renewal = true cost. The deal that wins *this* math is the genuine one. ## 5 rules for spotting a genuine deal (Diwali-season edition)

1. **Always question "up to".** The headline discount applies to the longest term. Verify your actual per-month price for *your* billing term at checkout.
2. **Look at the renewal price first, the discount second.** An 80% first-year discount means little if the renewal is 4x and you're staying for 3 years.
3. **Don't panic at countdown timers.** Flash-sale timers usually recycle. The substance is in the price, not the timer.
4. **Never forget GST and domain renewal.** 18% GST plus the second-year domain charge can add 15–20% to the true cost.
5. **Keep "expected" and "announced" separate.** Until a deal appears on the host's official site or an official announcement, don't build your plan around it — no matter how many sites quote its dates.
