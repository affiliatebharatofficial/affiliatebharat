## The straight answer: this comparison has changed

If you searched for **"hostgator vs milesweb hindi support"** and landed here, you need one fact before anything else — **this is no longer the comparison its name suggests.**

**HostGator India does not sell hosting to new customers any more.** On 29 October 2026, hostgator.in still shows its merger notice, and the page then redirects to BigRock automatically. So the real comparison today is:

- **MilesWeb** — which still claims Hindi support in writing
- **BigRock** — HostGator India's successor, which makes no Hindi claim on its official pages

This article is an English rewrite of our Hinglish comparison from 25 October, refreshed with a live check of all three official sites on **29 October 2026** — milesweb.in, bigrock.in and hostgator.in. The refresh matters: since the Hinglish article, MilesWeb's plan cards have started printing renewal prices, and BigRock's Linux hosting page now prints one too. Both changes are in the tables below.

**Honesty note:** this article is based on official pages and public data — no fake "we personally tested it" claims. We did not chat with any host's support team in Hindi, so where an official claim exists we call it a claim, and where no claim was found we say so plainly. Prices were verified on 29 October 2026; hosting companies change plans often.

HostGator.in still opens with a normal-looking homepage ("Powerful Web Hosting", starting at ₹69 a month), and then the merger popup appears under an "IMPORTANT UPDATE" banner dated 15 July 2025. ## The two-minute Hindi support test (do this before paying)

Do not trust any official claim blindly — whichever host you prefer. This test is free, and its result beats any review article, because it is your own experience:

1. **Open live chat** and write in Hindi: "Mujhe WordPress blog ke liye hosting chahiye, kaunsa plan sahi rahega?"
2. **See what language the reply comes in** — full Hindi, Hinglish, or an English reply asking you to write in English?
3. **Ask one technical question** — for example, "SSL free hai ya alag se charge hai?" — because sales answers and technical answers can differ in quality.
4. **Note the timing** — try chat late at night too, because "24/7" is only truly tested outside office hours.
