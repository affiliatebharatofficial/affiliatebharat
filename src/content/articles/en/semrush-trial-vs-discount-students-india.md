## The short answer: trial ≠ discount

First, clear up the confusion — **the Semrush free trial is not a discount.** A trial means using a paid plan free for 7 days, after which the full price is charged to your card unless you cancel. A discount means a cut in price — and on Semrush today there is exactly one discount: **up to 17% off with annual billing.**

## The Semrush free trial: 7 days, card required, auto-billing

Semrush's standard trial is **7 days**. It is available on the SEO, Starter and Pro+ plans; the Advanced plan has no trial, only a sales demo. During the trial you get the full limits of that plan — for example, on the SEO plan that means monitoring 5 websites and tracking 500 keywords a day.

But there are four trial rules that people often miss:

1. **A credit card is required.** According to Semrush's own blog and help centre, you have to enter card details to start a trial. Only the free account is card-free. Semrush says the trial needs a **physical card** — virtual cards are not accepted.
2. **It auto-bills.** If you do not cancel within 7 days, the trial turns into a paid subscription automatically — at the plan and billing period you chose at sign-up.
3. **Export is switched off.** During the trial, data export is disabled — you can see everything, but you cannot download reports. Finish your analysis inside the trial.
4. **You get it once.** The trial can be used only once per account. Some partner links claim a 14-day trial, but the standard on-site offer is 7 days — the old 30-day trial was discontinued in 2023.

**How to cancel:** go to Semrush's cancellation page and cancel, and do not forget to click the confirmation link that arrives by email — without that click the cancellation is not complete. According to third-party guides, cancelling an unpaid trial can take up to 3 business days to take effect, so do not wait for the last day — **cancel by day 5 or 6.**

### When should you take the trial?

Take the trial only when a week's worth of SEO work is already ready: a keyword list prepared, a list of competitor sites, and a site audit to run. If you start a trial empty-handed and spend 3 days deciding what to do, the 7 days will slip away — and on day 8, $139.95 will leave your card.

## For students: there is no student discount — these are the routes

"Semrush student discount" is one of the most searched questions about the tool, and the direct answer is: **there is no student discount for individual students.**

Some coupon sites claim a 30–50% discount through Student Beans, but that claim is conflicting — other independent coupon trackers state plainly that there is "no verified student discount available at this time". Semrush's own partner programme has no student coupon. So this article does not print that claim as a fact — it is unverified.

### Semrush for Education — but not for India

Semrush does run an official programme: **Semrush for Education.** It gives 3 months of free access, along with Semrush Academy courses and a certificate. More than 350 universities and 25,000+ students take part in it.

But its three conditions make it difficult for students in India:

1. **An individual student cannot apply.** Only a professor or department head applies for a partnership on behalf of their college.
2. **India is not on the eligible countries list.** The list covers the US, Canada, the UK, Australia, New Zealand and Europe.
3. **It lasts only 3 months.** After that, access ends.

So if you are a student in India, Semrush for Education is a closed route. You can still ask your college professor about it — there is no harm in asking — but do not rely on it.

### The real plan for students

1. **Free account + Google Search Console** — start at ₹0. GSC gives you your own site's real performance data (which keywords you rank for, which pages get clicks), and the Semrush free account gives you 10 competitor or keyword lookups a day.
2. **Semrush Academy's free courses** — even without buying the tool, you can take the free on-demand courses and earn certificates. They are useful on a CV.
3. **Take the trial only when the work is ready** — a week's worth of SEO work planned in advance, with a cancel reminder set.
4. **Budget alternative: Ubersuggest** — if the ₹0 stack is not enough, look at Ubersuggest Individual: $29/mo monthly, $192 a year ($16/mo) annual, or $290 once for lifetime. The free tier gives 3 searches a day, and the Chrome extension gives roughly 40 overlay searches a day. These figures are corroborated by multiple November 2026 readings.
