## Why the "₹149/mo" plan actually costs ₹449/mo at renewal

This is the most expensive sentence in Indian web hosting: "renews at ₹449/mo" — printed in small text under a giant "₹149/mo" headline. You paid ₹7,152 for four years of Hostinger Premium and did everything right. Then the renewal email arrives, and the next four years cost ₹21,552 before GST. That jump is not a surprise price hike. It was on the page the whole time, in the one line nobody reads.

This article exists so you read it. We verified Hostinger's renewal figures verbatim from hostinger.com/in plan cards, re-checked every alternative's official page on **20 October 2026**, and wrote down exactly where the numbers came from — and where they could not be confirmed. Nothing here is a "I personally tested it" story: this is official pricing pages, screenshots and public data, analysed honestly. Where sources conflicted, we say so out loud.

**Update vs the Hindi version:** if you read the Hinglish original (published 17 October), two things have moved since: Hostinger's homepage now leads with a live Black Friday banner, and MilesWeb's plan lineup and renewal claims have changed again. ## Three practical ways to dodge the renewal shock

**1. Lock the longest term on day one.** The intro rate applies to the entire term. A 48-month term gets you ₹149/mo for four full years; a 12-month term means a higher per-month rate *and* an early renewal. With a 30-day money-back guarantee, buying a short term "to test first" is weak logic.

**2. Migrate 30–45 days before renewal.** Free migration is commonly offered (MilesWeb does it). Mark the renewal date in your calendar the day you purchase — leaving auto-renewal on and forgetting about it is the single most expensive option.

**3. Pick a host with a predictable renewal structure.** Some hosts publish renewals clearly; some publish nothing. The alternatives below are ranked on exactly that.

If you are still choosing between Hostinger's own plans, read the [Single vs Premium detailed comparison](/en/hostinger-single-vs-premium-plan/) first — which plan fits your use case is a question you answer *before* the renewal maths.

- **There is no "same price at renewal" wording anywhere on the page.** A text search for "renewal" on the live page returns exactly one hit: the "MilesWeb vs Others" comparison table's **"Renewal price" row — ₹289/mo** (MilesWeb) vs ₹449/mo (Hostinger) vs ₹599/mo (GoDaddy), with the caveat *"Prices are indicative and based on publicly available information at the time of comparison."*

One honest caveat about our own process: a text-only fetch of the same page this morning *did* show a "Same price at renewal – Guaranteed" hero line. The live browser render — the thing an actual visitor sees — does not. When two reads of one page disagree, the browser wins, and the safe conclusion is that any "same renewal" claim here is unreliable right now. **Budget using the only concrete renewal number MilesWeb itself publishes: ₹289/mo** (their own table, labeled indicative) — not the ₹69–₹129 intro rates. What is still genuinely in MilesWeb's favour: 24/7 human support in English and Hindi, free domain, free SSL, daily backups, free expert migration, and an Indian company since 2012. On 20 October, a text-only fetch loaded a normal-looking HostGator India homepage with no notice at all. A live browser check on 20 October resolved it: **the page loads HostGator India content first, then a merger modal takes over** — top banner: "IMPORTANT UPDATE — Effective July 15, 2025, HostGator India and BigRock will be joining forces…", plus a modal headed "BigRock & HostGator India have joined forces" with a **"Redirecting to BigRock in 25 seconds…" countdown**, after which the page auto-redirects to bigrock.in (tagged `?utm_source=hgi&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=migration`) with the banner "You've been redirected from HostGator India."

The text fetch missed it because it does not wait out the 25-second countdown. Existing HostGator India customers should read their migration email for renewal terms. New buyers: BigRock's shared hosting starts at **₹69/mo** (65% OFF card), the page carries BigRock's own GTmetrix-backed speed table (June 2026), and — same gap as Bluehost — **renewal prices are not published on BigRock's page**.
