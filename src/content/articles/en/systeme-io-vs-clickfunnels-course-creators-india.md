You want to sell your first online course — in Hindi, to Indian students — and two names keep coming up: **Systeme.io** and **ClickFunnels**. Both call themselves "all-in-one": funnel builder, course hosting, email marketing, affiliate programme — everything in one place. But the price gap is enormous: one has a free plan that costs $0, the other starts at $97/month. And for India there is a second gap that matters even more than price: **whether your students will be able to pay by UPI.**

This is a straight comparison of the two — with figures verified live from the official pricing pages on 16 November 2026. No tool was bought or tested; this comparison is based on an analysis of public specifications, official help pages and independent reviews.

| Question | Systeme.io | ClickFunnels |
|-------|-----------|--------------|
| Free plan | **$0 — forever** (1 course, 500 students) | No free plan — 14-day trial only |
| Cheapest paid plan | **Startup $17/mo** | **Launch $97/mo** |
| Courses (entry plan) | 1 course (Free), 5 courses ($17/mo) | 3 courses ($97/mo) |
| Contacts (entry plan) | 2,000 (Free) / 5,000 ($17/mo) | 10,000 ($97/mo) |
| Transaction fees | **0% on every plan** | Gateway fees (Payments AI / Stripe) |
| Student payment in India | **Razorpay — UPI, netbanking, cards, INR** | **No Razorpay / Instamojo** — card or PayPal only |
| Best for | Solo course creators, first course | Large teams, funnels at scale |

The table above was verified from both official pricing pages on 16 November 2026. Now let us open up each point.

*Systeme.io's pricing page on 16 November 2026 — monthly billing view, all five plans visible*

- **Startup — $17/mo** (annual billing works out to $170 a year — 2 months free). 10 funnels, 10 automation rules, 3 custom domains, 5,000 contacts, 5 courses, 2,500 students.

**0% transaction fees** is also written on every plan — meaning Systeme.io takes nothing from each sale you make. The gateway (Stripe, PayPal or Razorpay) charges its own fees separately, but the platform takes no commission. ClickFunnels also takes no platform commission — but there the entry price itself is five times higher.

### One hidden limit on the Free plan

There is one limit that does not appear on the pricing page, and it is worth knowing: according to third-party reviews (citing systeme.io's help centre), **payment pages on a free account stop taking orders once lifetime sales reach $10,000**, until you move to a paid plan. A warning banner reportedly starts appearing at $8,000. This is reported — the pricing page does not mention it, so treat it as a reported limit rather than a confirmed fact. But in practice there is no reason to worry about it: $10,000 (roughly ₹8,80,000) in sales means your course is selling — and at that point paying $17/month is no burden at all.

## Course creator features: what do you actually get?

After price, the question is — what does each tool give you for building and selling a course?

**Courses and students.** Systeme.io includes a course builder on every plan: Free gives 1 course (500 students), Startup gives 5 (2,500 students), Webinar gives 20 (10,000 students), Unlimited is unlimited. ClickFunnels ties courses to the plan: Launch 3, Scale 6, Optimize 10, Dominate 20. That means 20 courses on ClickFunnels need the $5,997-a-year Dominate plan, while the same 20 courses on Systeme.io come with the $47/month Webinar plan. Both offer drip content, modules and lessons, and student management.

**Webinars.** Systeme.io's Webinar plan ($47/mo) says it in the name — 10 evergreen webinars are included. ClickFunnels also has webinar features, but they are not highlighted the same way and their limits on the entry plan are not clearly shown on the page. If your course sells through live or evergreen webinars, Systeme.io's $47/month plan is the direct answer.

**Funnels and email.** Both have a built-in funnel builder and email marketing. ClickFunnels' funnel builder is older and more mature — the template library and community are bigger, and the whole "funnel hacking" culture grew up around it. Systeme.io's builder is simpler, but for a beginner course creator simple is good — less to learn, faster to launch. Email limits differ: on Systeme.io, email sends are unlimited on every plan (contacts are limited, sends are not); on ClickFunnels, emails per month are capped — 50K on Launch, 300K on Scale, 750K on Optimize.

**Affiliate programme.** Both have built-in affiliate management — your students or partners can promote your course and earn a commission. Systeme.io itself pays its own affiliates 60% lifetime recurring commission (per its official affiliate page), which shows its affiliate system works in its own business. ClickFunnels' affiliate programme pays 30–40% recurring (public information). Both are useful for a course creator; the difference is only that on Systeme.io the affiliate feature is available even on the Free plan.

## The India angle: UPI, INR and payment gateways — the biggest difference

This section matters most for Indian course creators, because this is where the two tools genuinely differ.

**Systeme.io + Razorpay = you get UPI.** Razorpay is among systeme.io's supported payment gateways. At Razorpay checkout, your Indian students can pay **by UPI, netbanking, cards and wallets in INR**. Systeme.io's help pages say it supports INR, with some limits — for example, INR on PayPal is for domestic transactions only, and American Express is not supported on Stripe for INR transactions. Razorpay's own fees (around 2% + GST, third-party reported) apply separately, but your student gets to pay the way they want to. UPI accounts for more than 80% of digital payments in India — not having UPI means losing sales.

**ClickFunnels has no Razorpay or Instamojo.** ClickFunnels' official support page (March 2026) states clearly: *"Currently, we don't integrate with RazorPay and Instamojo."* Course payments go through ClickFunnels' Payments AI or Stripe — card, Apple Pay, PayPal. And Stripe India does not support UPI, netbanking or BNPL (per Stripe's own support page). So your Indian student cannot buy your course by UPI — they would need an international card or PayPal. Many Indian buyers do not have PayPal, and not everyone has a card with international transactions enabled.

**The subscription itself is also billed in dollars.** Neither tool accepts UPI or RuPay for its own subscription. You will need a Visa or Mastercard credit or debit card with international transactions enabled, and after bank conversion plus forex markup the price will come out slightly higher than the figure on the page. The only difference is that on Systeme.io $17/month means roughly ₹1,500/month, while on ClickFunnels $97/month means roughly ₹8,500/month — the forex markup applies to both, but it hurts less on the smaller amount.

## Which tool is for whom? A decision guide

- **First course, zero or low budget:** Systeme.io Free plan. Start without a card, sell 1 course to up to 500 students, then move to $17/month. No risk.
- **Course is selling, and you sell through webinars:** Systeme.io Webinar $47/mo. 20 courses, 10,000 students, 10 evergreen webinars — more courses than ClickFunnels' $197/month Scale plan, at a quarter of the price.
- **Indian audience, UPI is essential:** Systeme.io + Razorpay. This combination is the only one that gives you a UPI checkout. ClickFunnels does not offer this option at all.
- **Large team, agency, or multiple clients:** ClickFunnels Scale ($197/mo) or Optimize ($297/mo). 5–10 workspaces and team members, 75K–150K contacts — these team features only come with Systeme.io's Custom plan (priced on request).
- **Want funnel templates and community:** ClickFunnels' community and template library are bigger — if you want ready-made funnel templates and "funnel hacking" training, that is its strength. But you will pay a $97/month entry fee for it.
- **Want everything unlimited:** Systeme.io Unlimited $97/mo — the same price as ClickFunnels' entry plan, but with everything unlimited.

## The final word

Systeme.io and ClickFunnels are both good tools — but they are built for different people. ClickFunnels is a mature funnel platform built for large teams and businesses running at scale, and its price reflects that. Systeme.io is a solo creator's tool: start free, grow at $17/month, and take UPI payments from Indian students.

If this is your first course, spending $97/month to get started is not sensible — first validate your course on Systeme.io's free plan, and move to $17/month once the first sales come in. When your team grows and your sales are at scale, then consider ClickFunnels — by then you will know whether you actually need its advanced features or not. And whichever tool you choose, first take its free plan or trial and build one funnel for your course — a 14-day trial, one course test, and you will know for yourself which tool fits your work.
