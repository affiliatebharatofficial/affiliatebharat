## Checkout checklist: what actually decides the final price

1. **Apply the bank offer before trusting the price.** Amazon shows strikethrough prices; the 10% instant discount applies at payment. A ₹86,990 laptop can become ₹78,xxx — check the card-eligible price, not the listing.
2. **Cashback vs EMI is usually either/or.** Lenovo's terms: cashback is not valid on EMI transactions. Calculate both paths; the ₹999 warranty pack is often worth more than the interest saving.
3. **GSTIN at checkout for freelancers.** 18% GST is built into brand-store prices — add your GSTIN *before* paying to keep input tax credit. On marketplaces, verify the seller's GST invoice details pre-order.
4. **"Up to" exchange bonuses are ceilings.** Lenovo's ₹15,000 exchange bonus depends on your old device's condition and valuation — expect less than the headline.
5. **Prices move in hours, not days.** Every price in this article is from 17–22 October sources. The final checkout price is the only real price — a banner's percentage is advertising.
