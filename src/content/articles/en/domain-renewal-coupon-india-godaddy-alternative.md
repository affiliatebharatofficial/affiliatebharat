## ₹1 today, ₹1,599 every year after — the domain renewal trap

If you bought a GoDaddy .com for ₹1 (or ₹99), this article is for you. The domain industry's business model is the same as the hosting one we [covered in our Hostinger renewal analysis](/en/hostinger-renewal-price-increase-alternatives/): a cheap first-year sticker price, then list-price renewals from year two onward. GoDaddy's own Domain Registration Agreement states it verbatim — renewals bill at the **list (non-sale) price**, i.e. the number shown struck-through on the page.

We re-verified GoDaddy India's official .com page today, 24 October 2026, and nothing has changed:

## Why this matters more right now: two changes since our last look

Two things have changed since we first wrote about this on 18 October — and both raise the stakes of getting the renewal decision wrong.

**1. The .com wholesale price rises on 1 November 2026 — seven days away.** Verisign, which runs the .com registry, announced with its Q1 2026 results (23 April 2026) that the annual wholesale fee goes from **$10.26 to $10.97** — a 71-cent, roughly 7% increase. This is the first allowed increase since 2024 under Verisign's contract with ICANN, which permits up to four such 7% rises in the current cycle (running to October 2030). If Verisign takes all four at the maximum — it has taken every increase the contract has allowed since 2021 — wholesale .com reaches roughly $13.44 a year by 2030. Every registrar pays this floor for every .com, so the floor moves from **$10.46 to $11.17 a year** (wholesale + $0.20 ICANN fee). At-cost registrars like Cloudflare will pass it through one-for-one; the others will follow at their own pace. Under the registry contract, renewing before 1 November locks **today's wholesale price for every year you pay for** — up to ten years.

**2. GoDaddy renewals are no longer refundable.** On 15 October 2026, GoDaddy replaced its refund policy with a new definition: "Date of Purchase" now means the date of **initial** purchase and *specifically excludes* the date a renewal is processed. The replacement states it plainly: **"GoDaddy does not offer refunds on renewals (manual or auto-renewal)"** unless a product table or applicable law says otherwise. The old 45-day refund window on one-year domain auto-renewals is gone. (The change applies to customers who received GoDaddy's notice email of 15 September 2026; the standard 30-day annual and 48-hour monthly windows are unchanged.) This is the single most important shift for the strategy below: a domain that used to be reversible for 45 days after an auto-renewal is now final. Decide **before** the charge lands.

## Cloudflare Registrar: the cheapest, with one condition

Cloudflare Registrar sells .com at **cost** — the Verisign wholesale fee plus the $0.20 ICANN fee, zero markup. That is **$10.46/year** today, rising to **$11.17/year** after the 1 November wholesale increase. In Etemora's October 2026 nine-registrar comparison it is the cheapest renewal of the lot, with free WHOIS privacy included.

The condition: **the domain must use Cloudflare's DNS** — you cannot point it at your own nameservers. ## India-specific notes: GST, UPI and .in domains

- **18% GST.** GoDaddy India's invoices carry 18% GST. A ₹1,599 renewal becomes **~₹1,887** at checkout. Build your renewal budget on the GST-inclusive number, not the sticker. Our [GST invoice guide for hosting](/en/shared-hosting-small-business-gst-invoice-india/) covers the same trap in hosting.
- **UPI reality check.** GoDaddy India accepts UPI. Porkbun, Cloudflare and Namecheap need an international debit/credit card (or PayPal). If UPI is your only payment method, the practical play is staying at GoDaddy and combining the coupon test with a support-chat retention ask.
- **.in / .co.in.** GoDaddy India's .in renews at roughly ₹899/year and .co.in at roughly ₹749/year (per GoDaddy-derived aggregator pricing, October 2026; not re-verified today). For an India-targeted business a .in is perfectly sensible — but a .com carries global credibility, which is why this article prices .com first.
- **One flagged conflict.** A GoDaddy India resources-page table lists .com renewal at **₹1,499**, while the live .com product page (screenshotted above, re-verified today) shows **₹1,599**. We take the live product page as authoritative and recommend confirming on chat before the bill arrives.

## The transfer escape hatch — and the 1 November deadline

The coupon is a discount; the transfer is the structural fix. Under ICANN rules:

- The domain must be **60+ days old** (a 60-day lock follows registration or a previous transfer).
- A transfer is priced at the gaining registrar's **one-year renewal price** — and **adds a full year** to your registration. Moving to Porkbun costs about $11.08 and buys you that extra year.
- Steps: unlock the domain in your GoDaddy account → turn off WHOIS privacy → get the EPP authorisation code → initiate the transfer at the new registrar → approve the confirmation email. It takes 5–7 days.

**The timing is now genuinely urgent.** The wholesale price rises 1 November 2026 — seven days from today. Any renewal or transfer-year you pay for **before** that date is billed at today's wholesale floor; anything on or after it costs the higher one. If your renewal falls around November, renewing multi-year now — or transferring now — locks the lower price for those years.

## Who should do what: straight advice

- **UPI-only, staying with GoDaddy:** test GDBB3863C in the cart, then ask support chat for a retention discount 3–4 weeks before renewal. Use whichever works — they will not stack.
- **International card available, want permanent savings:** transfer to Porkbun ($11.08 flat), Dynadot ($10.88) or Cloudflare ($10.46 at-cost, DNS condition). Do it **before 1 November** to lock the current wholesale floor.
- **Beginner who wants the easiest dashboard:** Namecheap's first-year promo (~$6) is fine, but set a calendar reminder for the $18.48 renewal — and make the stay-or-transfer decision before the wholesale rise passes the price through.
- **Auto-renewal about to hit:** remember the policy change — since 15 October 2026, GoDaddy renewals are no longer refundable. Check your renewal dates in the account now; the decision has to happen **before** the charge.
