Your email list has reached 10,000 subscribers — congratulations, that is a real milestone. But it brings a question every blogger and creator worries about: **how much will the email marketing tool cost now?** The free plan's limit is over, and the paid plan's price grows with the list.

This article compares four popular tools directly — **Mailchimp, Brevo, GetResponse and Kit (formerly ConvertKit)** — at 10,000 subscribers, with prices verified live on 18 November 2026. No tool was bought or tested; this comparison is based on public specs, official pricing pages and independent reviews. It is the English version of our Hindi comparison published on 13 November 2026, refreshed with today's live prices — and almost every figure is unchanged, with two new details worth knowing.

| Tool (10,000 subscribers) | 1 email a month | 1 email a week | Free option |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mailchimp Standard** | ₹8,360/mo (₹4,180/mo in year one) | ₹8,360/mo (within the 120,000-send limit) | Free up to 250 contacts |
| **Brevo Starter** | ₹2,505/mo (20,000-email tier) | ~$46/mo (40,000-email tier) | ₹0 — 300 emails/day |
| **Brevo Standard** | ₹4,927/mo (20,000-email tier) | ~$97/mo (50,000-email tier) | — |
| **GetResponse Starter** | $79/mo (unlimited sends) | $79/mo (unlimited sends) | 14-day free trial |
| **Kit Free** | **$0** (broadcasts free) | **$0** (broadcasts free) | $0 up to 10,000 subscribers |
| **Kit Creator** | $139/mo (with automation) | $139/mo | — |

This table is based on prices verified live on 18 November 2026. Rupee figures come from the Indian pricing pages of Mailchimp and Brevo; dollar figures come from Kit's page and, for GetResponse, from the official page's November 2026 readings. Now let us look at each tool in turn.

## Mailchimp: the biggest name, the biggest bill

Mailchimp is the best-known name in email marketing — famous for templates, ecommerce integrations and automation flows. But at 10,000 contacts its bill is the highest of the four.

![Mailchimp's pricing page on 18 November 2026 — 5,001–10,000 contacts: Essentials ₹3,190/mo, Standard ₹4,180/mo, Premium ₹12,650/mo (first 12 months, 50% off); then ₹6,380, ₹8,360 and ₹25,300/mo](/screenshots/mailchimp-pricing-2026-en.png)
*Mailchimp's pricing page on 18 November 2026 — Indian visitors see rupee prices, 5,001–10,000 contacts band, 50% off for the first 12 months*

On today's live page (Indian visitor, rupee currency), in the 5,001–10,000 contacts band:

- **Essentials — ₹3,190 a month for the first 12 months, then ₹6,380 a month.** Basic automation and scheduling, but no multi-step automation.
- **Standard — ₹4,180 a month for the first 12 months, then ₹8,360 a month.** Multi-step automation, send-time optimisation and behavioural targeting. Monthly allowance: **120,000 emails a month** (12 times your contacts).
- **Premium — ₹12,650 a month for the first 12 months, then ₹25,300 a month.** Advanced segmentation, multivariate testing and phone support — this is for larger teams.

The screenshot confirms the figures are identical to the 13 November check — nothing has moved in five days. Two things are worth noting. First, the page says **"Save 50%"** — the older guides that mention a 15% discount are describing a different offer. Second, that 15% figure is not imaginary: on the US dollar page, the same Standard plan is **$135 a month** (multiple November 2026 readings agree) and the page offers 15% off for the first 12 months to businesses with 10,000+ contacts. So the discount you are offered depends on which currency the page shows you — the Indian page gives 50% off in rupees, the US page gives 15% off in dollars. Check the final amount at checkout either way.

There is another side to Mailchimp's billing that pushes the bill up: it charges on **stored contacts** — unsubscribed and non-subscribed contacts count too until you archive them, and the bill is based on the month's peak contact total. One person in two audiences counts twice. Keeping your list clean saves real money here.

**Annual cost (Standard):** at full price, ₹8,360 × 12 = **₹1,00,320 a year**; in the first year at 50% off, ₹50,160. In dollars, $135 × 12 = $1,620 a year.

## Brevo: cheapest if you send rarely, pricier if you send often

Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) bills differently — it charges for **emails sent**, not contacts stored. That is why it is the cheapest option for low-frequency senders and gets expensive for high-frequency ones.

![Brevo's pricing page on 18 November 2026 — "Customize your plan" calculator, Starter tab, 20,000 emails/month, yearly billing: total ₹2,256.33/month (₹2,505.00 with monthly billing); 500,000 contacts included](/screenshots/brevo-pricing-2026-en.png)
*Brevo's pricing page on 18 November 2026 — the calculator at 20,000 emails a month, yearly billing, Starter total ₹2,256.33 a month*

On today's live page (Indian visitor, rupee currency), with the "Customize your plan" calculator set to 20,000 emails a month:

- **Starter — ₹2,505 a month on monthly billing, ₹2,256.33 a month on yearly billing (−10%).** 20,000 emails a month, basic automation.
- **Standard — ₹4,927 a month on monthly billing, ₹4,433.92 a month on yearly billing (−10%).** Unlimited automation, A/B testing, web tracking and advanced features.
- **Free — ₹0, forever.** With a 300-emails-a-day limit.

**Here is the trap most buyers miss:** Brevo's smaller email tiers also cap how many contacts you can store — 500 contacts on the 5,000-email tier, 1,500 on 10,000 emails, 2,500 on 15,000 emails. Your list is 10,000 contacts, so the 10,000-email tier ($17 a month) is no use to you — you have to take the **20,000-email tier**, where the contact cap jumps to 500,000. So "one email a month = ₹2,505" is your entry point, not less.

The entry tier itself is worth a line too: at 5,000 emails a month, Starter costs ₹625 a month on monthly billing (₹562.50 on yearly billing), read live today from the plan cards. That is the cheapest paid starting point on any of the four tools — but it only stores 500 contacts, so it cannot hold your list.

In US dollars (multiple November 2026 readings): Starter is $32 a month and Standard $69 a month at 20,000 emails; at 40,000 emails Starter is about $46 and Standard needs the 50,000-email tier at about $97. Rupee prices are again lower than a straight dollar conversion — Brevo Standard at ₹4,927 compares with $69 × 88 = ₹6,072.

**Annual cost:** Starter on monthly billing is ₹2,505 × 12 = **₹30,060 a year** (₹27,076 on yearly billing); Standard is ₹4,927 × 12 = **₹59,124 a year** (₹53,207 on yearly billing).

## GetResponse: the middle road with unlimited sends

GetResponse charges by contacts, but gives **unlimited monthly email sends on every plan**. That is why it beats Brevo for people who send often.

![GetResponse's pricing page on 18 November 2026 — list size 1,000, yearly billing (−18%), in INR: Starter ₹1,466.24/mo (₹1,788.10/mo monthly), Marketer ₹4,553.07/mo, Creator ₹5,324.78/mo](/screenshots/getresponse-pricing-inr-2026-en.png)
*GetResponse's pricing page on 18 November 2026 — showing rupee prices to Indian visitors at the 1,000-contact list size, yearly billing*

Unlike on 13 November, when the page timed out three times in the live browser, GetResponse's pricing page loaded on the first attempt today. One new detail: it now shows **rupee prices to Indian visitors**. At the 1,000-contact list size, on yearly billing: Starter ₹1,466.24 a month (₹1,788.10 monthly), Marketer ₹4,553.07 a month (₹5,552.52 monthly) and Creator ₹5,324.78 a month (₹6,493.63 monthly). Note that the screenshot shows the 1,000-contact size — the 10,000-contact rupee price was not captured, so the dollar figures below are used for the comparison at 10,000 contacts. The small difference from the 2 November reading (Starter ₹1,479.43 a year) is exchange-rate movement; the page itself notes that prices are net amounts and may vary with exchange rates.

At 10,000 contacts, the dollar figures are the same in every independent reading of the official pricing page in November 2026, most recently re-checked on 12 November:

- **Starter — $79 a month on monthly billing, $64.78 a month on yearly billing (−18%).** 10,000 contacts, unlimited sends, 1 automation workflow, landing page builder, AI tools.
- **Marketer — $114 a month, $93.48 a month on yearly billing.** Unlimited automation workflows, abandoned cart recovery, sales funnels.
- **Creator — $134 a month, $109.88 a month on yearly billing.** Webinars, course creator, paid newsletters — for creators.
- **Free account + 14-day trial:** premium features free for 14 days with no card; after that the free account's limits apply.

One more thing: if your list grows past your tier (new subscribers arriving through forms or integrations), GetResponse charges a **list extension fee** — the price difference to the next tier, every month, until you remove the extra contacts. Keeping your list right at the edge of a tier can get expensive.

**Annual cost (Starter):** on monthly billing, $79 × 12 = **$948 a year** (roughly ₹83,400 at ₹88 to the dollar — approximate); on yearly billing, $64.78 × 12 = **$777.36 a year**.

## Kit: free up to 10,000 subscribers, $139 a month for automation

Kit (formerly ConvertKit) is built for creators — newsletters, automation and selling digital products, all in one place. Its biggest selling point: **the Free plan costs $0 up to 10,000 subscribers.**

![Kit's pricing page on 18 November 2026 — yearly billing, 10,000 subscribers: Free $0, Creator $116/mo ($1,390/year), Pro $119/mo first year (then $158/mo)](/screenshots/kit-pricing-10000-2026-en.png)
*Kit's pricing page on 18 November 2026 — yearly billing, 10,000 subscribers slider, Pro first-year offer still live*

On today's live page (US dollars — Kit shows dollars even to Indian visitors), with 10,000 subscribers selected:

- **Free — $0 a month.** Up to 10,000 subscribers, unlimited landing pages, forms and broadcasts, audience tagging, selling digital products. But **no Visual Automations and no automated sequences**, only 1 user, and Kit branding stays on.
- **Creator — $139 a month on monthly billing, $116 a month on yearly billing ($1,390 a year, $278 saved).** Unlimited Visual Automations and sequences, A/B subject lines, polls, SMS, branding removal, 2 users.
- **Pro — $189 a month on monthly billing; $158 a month on yearly billing ($1,890 a year) as the regular price, and $119 a month ($1,418 for the first year, $850 saved) under the "EXTRA 25% OFF FIRST YEAR" offer.** Pro adds Subscriber Signals (engagement scoring), the Growth Concierge team and unlimited users.

One change since the Hindi original: the Pro offer's end date has moved — it said 7 November when we checked on 5 November, and now, re-checked on 9 November, it runs through **30 November 2026**. So the offer is still live today, but only for twelve more days; after that, Pro renews at the standard $158 a month yearly rate.

Kit's catch is clear: the free plan is enough for building a list and sending newsletters, but **the first welcome sequence is the trigger to upgrade**. Going from free to Creator at 10,000 subscribers is a jump from $0 straight to $139 a month — there is no gradual ramp in between. Selling digital products also carries a **3.5% + 30¢ fee per sale** on every plan.

**Annual cost (Creator):** on monthly billing, $139 × 12 = **$1,668 a year**; on yearly billing, **$1,390 a year**.

## Sending frequency changes the winner

This is the most important part of the comparison. The same list, three different sending patterns — and the winner changes every time:

| Pattern (10,000 contacts) | Brevo Starter | Brevo Standard | Mailchimp Standard | GetResponse Starter | Kit Creator |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 email a month (10,000 sends)** | ₹2,505/mo | ₹4,927/mo | ₹8,360/mo | $79/mo | $139/mo |
| **1 email a week (40,000 sends)** | ~$46/mo | ~$97/mo | ₹8,360/mo | $79/mo | $139/mo |
| **Daily email (~300,000 sends)** | very expensive | ~$309/mo | ~$310/mo (25,000-contact tier) | **$79/mo** | $139/mo |

(US dollar figures are third-party readings, October–November 2026; Brevo's and Mailchimp's high-volume figures come from DIMA Post's 2 November 2026 reading.)

The pattern is clear: **send rarely, Brevo wins; send often, GetResponse wins.** Mailchimp's Standard plan runs at one price up to 120,000 sends a month — fine for people sending two or three emails a week — but its base price is already high. Kit's bill never rises with sends, but its base price of $139 a month is high — right for automation, wrong for newsletters only.

## INR vs USD: which price will you see?

Today's live check showed an interesting difference: **Mailchimp and Brevo show Indian visitors rupee prices, Kit shows dollars, and GetResponse now shows rupees too** (at least at the 1,000-contact size). And the rupee prices are lower than what you get by multiplying the dollar price by ₹88 — Mailchimp Standard's full price is ₹8,360 while $135 × 88 = ₹11,880; Brevo Standard is ₹4,927 while $69 × 88 = ₹6,072.

That means dollar figures in older guides can overstate what an Indian reader will actually pay. Always open the official pricing page in your own browser to see which currency it shows you — and confirm the final amount, with taxes, at checkout. GetResponse showed Indian visitors rupee prices on 2 November too, but its 10,000-contact rupee price could not be verified today, so check it at checkout.

## Which tool is for whom?

- **Newsletter only, zero budget:** **Kit Free ($0).** Broadcasts are free up to 10,000 subscribers. Move to Creator ($139 a month) when you need a welcome sequence or automation.
- **One or two emails a month, lowest cost:** **Brevo Starter (₹2,505 a month).** But remember — the bill rises as frequency rises.
- **Automation + ecommerce, daily email:** **GetResponse Starter ($79 a month).** Unlimited sends make it the cheapest paid option at high volume, and Marketer ($114 a month) adds abandoned cart and sales funnels.
- **Mailchimp Standard (₹8,360 a month):** buy it only when you specifically need its templates, integrations or ecommerce ecosystem. On price alone it is the most expensive.
- **Creators selling courses or digital products:** Kit Creator ($139 a month) or GetResponse Creator ($134 a month) — both have selling tools built in; Kit's free plan also lets you validate first before paying.

And one practical tip: **keep your list clean.** Mailchimp and GetResponse both charge on stored contacts — archiving inactive or unsubscribed contacts directly lowers the bill. On Brevo, count your sends — every extra email costs money.

## The final word

At 10,000 subscribers, the cheapest way to send a newsletter is free — Kit's Free plan — or nearly free, with Brevo Starter at ₹2,505 a month. The moment you need automation, the maths changes: GetResponse Starter at $79 a month with unlimited sends is the best-value paid plan, and Kit Creator at $139 a month is the creator-focused alternative. Mailchimp Standard at ₹8,360 a month full price is the most expensive of the four, and only makes sense if you need what only Mailchimp offers. Whatever you choose, count your sending frequency before you buy — that one number decides which tool is actually cheapest for you.
