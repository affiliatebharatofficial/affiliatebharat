If you are starting a blog in Hindi, your hosting choice matters more than most English guides admit. You need affordable pricing in rupees, support you can actually talk to in Hindi, and renewal prices that don't ambush you in year two. This comparison is based on analysis of public specs, user reviews and pricing data verified on 2026-10-09 — not on personal testing.

## Hostinger vs Bluehost India: verified price comparison

Here is what the official pricing pages showed when we checked them:

| | Hostinger Single | Hostinger Premium | Bluehost India Basic | Bluehost India Choice Plus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Intro price** | ₹69/mo (48-mo term) | ₹149/mo (48-mo term) | ₹299/mo (36-mo term) | ₹499/mo (36-mo term) |
| **Renewal price** | ₹289/mo | ₹449/mo | Not published | Not published |
| **Websites** | 1 | 3 | 1 | Unlimited |
| **Storage** | 10 GB SSD | 20 GB SSD | 50 GB | Unmetered |
| **Free domain** | Yes | Yes (1 yr) | Yes | Yes |

Two honest observations:

1. **Hostinger's ₹69/mo is real but temporary.** After 48 months you pay ₹289/mo. Still reasonable, but budget for it.
2. **Bluehost India does not publish renewal prices** on its official help page. That is a genuine transparency problem — you are buying without knowing what year two costs.

## The Hindi support question (often ignored)

Most comparison sites never ask this. We did. Here is what is verifiable:

- **MilesWeb**: officially advertises "Support in English or Hindi, 24/7." Verified.
- **Hostinger**: Hindi support not confirmed on official India pages. The panel is easy to use, but if you need to call someone and explain a problem in Hindi, that is not a promised service.
- **Bluehost India**: Hindi support not confirmed on official pages either.

If your English is comfortable, this may not matter to you. But for many first-time Hindi bloggers — especially those whose parents' generation runs the family business blog — being able to get help in Hindi is the difference between fixing a problem in 20 minutes and abandoning the site.

## Which plan for which kind of Hindi blog?

**Personal Hindi blog / shayari / poetry site:** Hostinger Single (₹69/mo intro) or MilesWeb Starter (₹69/mo, no renewal shock). One site, 10 GB storage — plenty for text and images.

**Hindi news or multi-topic blog:** Hostinger Premium (₹149/mo intro, 3 sites, free domain 1 year) if you want the big-brand ecosystem; MilesWeb Business (₹199/mo, 50 sites, 100 GB NVMe) if you want predictable long-term costs.

**YouTuber companion blog / affiliate site:** The free domain bundled with Hostinger Premium saves you ~₹900–1,200/year on domain registration, which matters when you are counting every rupee.

## The renewal trap: a worked example

Say you buy Hostinger Single for 48 months at ₹69/mo. You pay roughly ₹3,312 + GST upfront. In month 49, your plan renews at ₹289/mo — **4.2x the intro price**. That is not a scam; it is how the industry works. But many beginners discover it only when the renewal email arrives.

Bluehost India's unpublished renewals are worse in one way: you cannot even plan. At least Hostinger tells you the number.

MilesWeb's model — ₹69/mo forever — is the honest outlier. If you hate surprises, it is worth serious consideration despite the smaller brand.
