## The straight answer first: Basic and Choice Plus are gone

If you searched for **"bluehost india basic vs choice plus which plan"** and landed here, you need one fact before anything else — **neither plan is sold anymore.**

On 30 October 2026, Bluehost's official India site was checked live, and this is what it showed:

- The old URL `bluehost.in/hosting/shared-hosting` returns a **404**.
- `bluehost.in` redirects to `bluehost.com/in`.
- The shared hosting page now sells **Starter, Business, and eCommerce Essentials** — there is no Basic, Plus, or Choice Plus plan card.
- The only place an old name survives is one line in the Domain Manager section mentioning "Plus and Business Pro accounts" — that is not a plan you can buy.

So the question this article answers has changed. It is no longer "Basic or Choice Plus?" but **"I would have bought Basic — what do I buy now? And I would have bought Choice Plus — what replaces it?"** That is what follows, with prices re-verified today.

This analysis is based on public specs, user reviews, and official pricing data. We have not bought and personally tested Bluehost's plans, so you will find no "I tested it" claims here — only what the official page actually showed.

## A warning before the prices: Bluehost showed two price sets today

This is the most important thing in this article, and it is unusual enough to put up front. On 30 October 2026, the same official page, on the same Standard tab, served **two different price sets on successive loads**. Websites, storage, and terms were identical in both — only the prices and discount badges changed:

| Plan (36-month term) | Main price set (final load) | Second price set (earlier load) |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | ₹383/mo, renews ₹960/mo (Save 60%) | ₹199/mo, renews ₹349/mo (Save 43%) |
| Business | ₹672/mo, renews ₹1,345/mo (Save 50%) | ₹279/mo, renews ₹529/mo (Save 47%) |
| eCommerce Essentials | ₹1,441/mo, renews ₹2,113/mo (Save 32%) | ₹649/mo, renews ₹1,299/mo (Save 50%) |

The most likely explanation is that Bluehost is A/B testing prices, or rolling out a new promotion unevenly. We cannot tell you which set you will see — that depends on the version the site serves you. So this article uses the main set (the final load, which also matches the 27 October verification) as its headline figures, and flags the second set wherever it matters. **Before you pay, open the page yourself and read the card in front of you, then check the renewal figure at checkout.** Any article that quotes a single Bluehost price today without this warning is hiding something.

## Old plans vs new plans: the mapping

First, what the old plans were. These figures were verified on Bluehost's official help page on 9 October 2026; the plans are no longer sold, so they are shown here only for mapping:

| Old plan | Intro price (36-mo) | Websites | Storage | Notable feature |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | ₹299/mo | 1 | 50 GB | 5 email accounts |
| Choice Plus | ₹499/mo | Unlimited | Unmetered | Domain privacy + CodeGuard backup |

And today's new plans (30 October 2026, main price set, official page):

| New plan | Intro price (36-mo) | Renewal | Websites | Storage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | ₹383/mo | ₹960/mo | 10 | 10 GB NVMe |

The mapping is straightforward:

- **Basic → Starter.** The Basic buyer was a one-website blogger. Starter is its replacement — and on websites it is actually better (10 instead of 1), but on storage it is worse (10 GB NVMe instead of 50 GB).
- **Choice Plus → Business.** The Choice Plus buyer ran multiple sites. Business is the closest successor — 50 websites, domain privacy free for the first year, weekly backups, and AI malware protection. The "unlimited websites" promise is gone, but 50 sites is plenty for almost any individual user.

One genuine improvement: the old plans' renewal prices were **never published** on the official site. The new plans print the renewal price right on the card. A buyer today can see the future bill before paying — as long as they also notice which of the two price sets they were served.

## Each new plan in detail: who it is for

### Starter — ₹383/mo (renews ₹960/mo): for your first blog

The Starter card reads: "Perfect for your first website or blog." It includes:

- 10 websites, 10 GB NVMe SSD storage, ideal for up to 40,000 visits a month
- Free domain for the first year, free Let's Encrypt SSL, weekly website backups
- Free CDN, managed WordPress updates, 99.99% uptime SLA, 30-day money-back guarantee
- **Domain privacy is not listed on Starter** — that gap is filled only by Business

**Who it is for:** One or two blogs, a portfolio site, or a small business site. The 10 GB storage limit is the thing to watch: fine for a text-heavy blog, but it can fill up fast if your site carries heavy images or video.

**Three-year total:** ₹383 × 36 = ₹13,788 on the intro term. At renewal it becomes ₹960 a month — a 2.5x jump. On the second price set seen today, the same maths would be ₹199 × 36 = ₹7,164 intro and ₹349 a month at renewal. That gap is exactly why you must check which set you are being shown.

### Business — ₹672/mo (renews ₹1,345/mo): for multi-site users

Business carries the "MOST POPULAR" tag. It includes:

- 50 websites, 50 GB NVMe SSD storage, ideal for up to 200,000 visits a month
- Domain privacy **free for the first year**, weekly backups
- AI-powered malware detection and removal, web application firewall, DDoS protection
- Free domain for the first year, free SSL, 30-day money-back guarantee

**Who it is for:** Bloggers running more than one site, anyone managing client sites, or anyone who wants their name and number hidden from WHOIS records (that is what domain privacy does). This is the natural path for old Choice Plus users — with the honest caveat that "unlimited websites" has become 50.

**Three-year total:** ₹672 × 36 = ₹24,192 on the intro term, renewing at ₹1,345 a month — a 2x jump. On the second price set: ₹279 × 36 = ₹10,044 intro, ₹529 a month at renewal.

### eCommerce Essentials — ₹1,441/mo (renews ₹2,113/mo): for online stores only

- 100 websites, 100 GB NVMe storage, ideal for up to 400,000 visits a month
- All of Business's security features, plus WooCommerce tools: secure payment processing, WooCommerce auto-install, product subscriptions, visitor memberships, paid courses, affiliate program
- Domain privacy free for the first year, weekly backups

**Who it is for:** Only buy this if you genuinely run an online store — selling courses, memberships, or subscriptions. For a blog alone it is overkill; Business plus separate tools will cost you less. On the second price set seen today, this plan was ₹649 a month renewing at ₹1,299 — again, verify at checkout.

## The sale, the discount badges, and the GST question

Three honest notes on things the page does not make clear:

1. **The page title still says "Save 70%", but the cards say 60%, 50%, and 32%** (or 43%, 47%, and 50% on the second price set). We have quoted the card figures throughout, because those are what you see when buying. The 70% headline does not match any card.
2. **No sale banner or sale terms were visible on today's page.** On 27 October, a site-wide sale (1 October to 30 November 2026) with a bundle discount was described in a terms dialog; on 30 October, no banner, dates, or bundle terms appeared on the page at all. So this article makes no bundle-discount claim — if a discount is applied at checkout, that is the only place you can confirm it.
3. **GST is genuinely unclear.** A text fetch of the page showed "GST not included" footnotes under each card, but the rendered page viewed in a browser showed no GST note on the cards. The 27 October check had found only a generic "taxes and fees extra" line. Until Bluehost prints one consistent statement, do not assume the card price is the final price — read the tax line at checkout, and if you need a GST invoice for a business, confirm it with support first. ## Which plan should you buy: a decision guide

- **Starting one blog on a tight budget → Starter.** Watch the 10 GB storage limit, and compress images before uploading.
- **Running 2–10 sites, or you want domain privacy → Business.** This is today's most balanced plan, and the true successor to Choice Plus.
- **Selling online — courses, memberships, subscriptions → eCommerce Essentials.** Do not buy it just for a blog.
- **Site already heavy and CPU-bound → High Performance tab's Pro.** Go higher only when traffic genuinely demands it.

And one practical rule before you commit: **work out the renewal price before choosing a 36-month term.** Starter becomes ₹960 a month after three years on the main price set (or ₹349 on the second set — the difference shows why checking matters). If you think you might switch hosts within three years, consider a shorter term, knowing the monthly price will be higher — the checkout page will show you.
