"How much does a WordPress blog cost in India?" is one of the most-searched blogging questions — and most answers are either vague or sponsored. Here is an itemized, honest breakdown using prices verified on 2026-10-09. This is based on analysis of public specs and pricing data, not personal testing.

## The short answer

| Cost item | Year 1 (typical) | Renewal / year 2+ |
|---|---|---|
| Domain (.com) | ₹0–1,200 (often free with hosting) | ₹900–1,200 |
| Hosting (shared) | ₹830–1,800 (intro pricing) | ₹830–7,800 (depends on host) |
| Theme | ₹0 (free theme) | ₹0 |
| Plugins | ₹0 (free tiers) | ₹0 |
| SSL certificate | ₹0 (included) | ₹0 |
| **Total realistic range** | **~₹4,000–6,000** | **~₹5,000–8,000** |

WordPress.org software itself is **free, forever**. You are paying for exactly two things: a domain name and web hosting. Everything else is optional.

## Item 1: Domain name — ₹0 to ₹1,200/year

A domain is your blog's address (yourblog.com). Typical .com pricing in India:

- **Paid separately:** ~₹900–1,200/year from registrars.
- **Free with hosting:** Hostinger Premium and above include a free domain for 1 year; MilesWeb plans include a free domain too. Hostinger's cheapest Single plan also carries a free-domain banner on yearly plans.

**Honest tip:** Take the free domain in year one, but note it renews at the normal ~₹900–1,200 rate. There is no such thing as a permanently free domain.

## Item 3: Theme — ₹0 to start

You do not need a paid theme. Excellent free options:

- **Astra / GeneratePress / Kadence (free versions):** fast, clean, and perfectly good for a new blog.
- Paid themes (₹3,000–5,000 one-time) only make sense once your blog earns or you need specific design features.

**Rule of thumb:** if your blog makes ₹0, your theme should cost ₹0.

## Item 4: Plugins — ₹0 to start

The essential free plugins cover everything a beginner needs:

- **Rank Math / Yoast SEO (free):** on-page SEO.
- **Wordfence (free):** security.
- **UpdraftPlus (free):** backups.
- **WPForms Lite / Contact Form 7:** contact forms.
- **LiteSpeed Cache / WP Super Cache:** speed.

Premium plugins are a "later" expense. Many successful Indian blogs run entirely on free plugins for their first year.

## Item 5: SSL — ₹0

Every host in our comparison includes free SSL (MilesWeb verified; Hostinger includes it on its plans). Never pay extra for basic SSL in 2026.

## The renewal trap, explained with numbers

This is the part most "blog cost" articles skip. Let us trace a Hostinger Single buyer:

- **Months 1–48:** ₹69/mo → ~₹3,312 + GST paid upfront.
- **Month 49 onward:** ₹289/mo → **₹3,468/year + GST**, a 4.2x jump.

Nothing dishonest about it — the renewal price is published — but beginners routinely budget year-one prices for year two and get shocked.

The MilesWeb buyer, by contrast, pays ₹69/mo (~₹977/year with GST) in year one *and* year two, because the same-price-at-renewal is guaranteed. Over 4 years, the total cost difference is dramatic:

| | 4-year total (approx, excl. GST) |
|---|---|
| Hostinger Single (48-mo intro, then renewal rate) | ~₹3,312 (all intro — renewal hits in year 5) |
| MilesWeb Starter | ~₹3,312 (₹828 × 4, same rate throughout) |

Interesting — over exactly 4 years they tie, because Hostinger's 48-month intro covers the whole period. The trap only bites if you buy a *shorter* Hostinger term (12 or 24 months) and then renew at ₹289/mo. Always match the term length to your commitment.

## Three realistic budgets

**Minimum viable blog (~₹1,000–2,000/year):** MilesWeb Starter + free domain + free theme + free plugins. Honestly enough for 90% of beginners.

**Comfortable starter (~₹4,000–6,000 year 1):** Hostinger Premium 12-month (~₹1,788 + GST) + free domain year 1 + free theme/plugins. Room for 3 sites if you experiment.

**Growing blog (~₹8,000–12,000/year):** MilesWeb Business or Hostinger Unlimited at renewal rates + paid theme + one premium plugin (e.g., Rank Math Pro). Only spend this when traffic or income justifies it.

## Costs you can skip (beginner traps)

- **Paid logo design** — use a free tool; your content matters more.
- **Premium stock photos** — free libraries are fine.
- **"SEO services" for a new blog** — learn basic on-page SEO yourself first.
- **Multiple paid plugins** — each one slows your site and empties your wallet.

## Bottom line

Starting a WordPress blog in India genuinely costs **₹1,000–6,000 in year one**, and the software is free. Your two real decisions are the domain (take the free-with-hosting offer) and the host — where the only question that matters is *"what will I pay in year two?"* Answer that before you buy, and you will never feel cheated.

And remember that hosting is only the beginning of the journey, not the end. The bloggers who succeed are not the ones who found the cheapest plan — they are the ones who published consistently for a year while their competitors debated themes and plugins. Keep your costs low, your publishing schedule steady, and upgrade your hosting only when your traffic genuinely demands it.
