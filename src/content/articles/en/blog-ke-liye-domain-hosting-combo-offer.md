## "Free domain + hosting" — understand the offer, then buy it

Every beginner starting a blog faces the same question: **should I buy the domain separately, or take it in a combo offer with hosting?** Every hosting company splashes a "FREE DOMAIN" banner across its homepage, but the small print under that banner is what decides the real deal.

For this article we re-verified both official pages live on **28 October 2026** — hostinger.com/in and milesweb.in — and worked out the full combo maths: the real first-year cost, the second-year renewal, GST, and the gotchas that only surface at checkout. It is an English rewrite of our Hinglish combo guide from 23 October, refreshed with today's prices — and the refresh matters, because MilesWeb's lineup has changed again since then.

**Honesty note:** this article is based on official pricing pages and public data — no fake "we personally tested it" claims. Where an official page contradicts itself, both sides are printed. Prices were verified on 28 October 2026; hosting companies change plans often, and you will see a live example of that below.

## The truth about "free domain": free for one year only

Clear this up first, because it is the biggest misunderstanding in blog hosting:

- **Free domain = the first year's registration fee is ₹0.** The domain is registered in your name and managed in your hosting account.
- **From year two, you pay the renewal** — at the standard registrar rate (₹800–1,600 a year, depending on extension).
- **60-day transfer lock:** under ICANN rules, a new or transferred domain cannot move to another registrar for the first 60 days. If you plan to shift to a cheaper registrar at renewal, think about it early.
- **Extension limits:** the free domain does not cover every extension. Hostinger's qualifying extensions are .com, .net, .org and .in (from third-party sources; the official page publishes no exact list). MilesWeb publishes no extension list on its official page — review sites name .com, .in and .co.in.

## The real 2-year cost comparison (GST included)

Never decide on the first-year headline price — look at two years:

| Combo | Year 1 (upfront + GST) | Year 2 (hosting renewal + domain renewal, GST included) | 2-year total (approx) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hostinger Premium + free domain | ₹7,873 (48-mo) | ₹6,358 (₹449×12+GST) + ~₹943 domain | **~₹15,170** |
| Hostinger Single + domain bought separately | ~₹4,900 (48-mo + domain) | ₹4,092 (₹289×12+GST) + ~₹943 domain | **~₹9,935** |

MilesWeb's year-2 number is the lowest only if the same-renewal claim holds — and the conflict above is exactly why we show both versions. **This is why our rule is "renewal unpublished = keep a buffer in the budget."**

BigRock (HostGator India's successor) shows shared hosting at ₹69/mo, but its renewal is unpublished on the official page, so its 2-year maths is a guess — marked "unknown" in the table. Think twice before a long term with a host that will not state its renewal.

## The third route: domain separate, hosting separate

A combo is not best for everyone. If you are a little technical, this split strategy is often cheaper:

1. **Buy the domain from Porkbun or Cloudflare** — a .com renews at a flat ~$11 (roughly ₹920) at Porkbun, and near-wholesale at Cloudflare. GoDaddy's .com renewal is about ₹1,599 a year — nearly double. (We covered this in detail in our [domain renewal guide](/en/domain-renewal-coupon-india-godaddy-alternative/).)
2. **Buy hosting wherever it is cheapest** — MilesWeb Starter or Hostinger Single.
3. **Point the domain's DNS at the host's nameservers** — a 10-minute job, with steps in every host's knowledge base.

The trade-off: 10–15 extra minutes of setup and two dashboards to manage. The payoff: from year two, domain renewal is always cheap, and if you ever switch hosts, the domain is already under your control.

## 5-point checklist before taking a combo

1. **Which plans carry the free domain** — at Hostinger, Single is excluded; at MilesWeb, Starter carries it today (keep verifying — plans change).
2. **Is the renewal price printed on the card?** — printed means the budget is firm; unpublished means keep a buffer.
3. **Add GST** — both hosts state prices exclude 18% GST; the ₹7,873 Premium figure only appears at checkout.
4. **Term length vs refund** — a 36/48-month upfront payment is large; both offer a 30-day money-back guarantee (the domain fee itself is non-refundable — an industry standard).
5. **Remember the 60-day lock** — a free domain cannot transfer for the first 60 days; plan renewal around that.
