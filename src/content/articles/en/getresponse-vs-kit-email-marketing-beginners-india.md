Every beginner starting email marketing faces the same question: start free or pay — and with which tool? In India, two names come up more than any others: **GetResponse** (an all-in-one marketing platform) and **Kit** (formerly ConvertKit, built for creators).

Both have free plans, both price paid plans by subscriber count, and both make big claims. This guide sticks to facts verified from official pricing pages and help docs — no "we personally tested it" claims, because we have not bought and run both tools as a customer.

**Honesty note:** This article is based on an analysis of public pricing pages, official FAQ and help pages, and independent pricing trackers. Where the official page did not confirm something, it is labelled as reported, not stated as fact.

## Research methodology

Headline prices were re-verified on 2 November 2026 on the official pricing pages: GetResponse's pricing page (1,000-contact band, monthly and yearly billing) and Kit's pricing page (1,000-subscriber band, monthly and yearly billing, plus the 5,000 and 10,000 bands via the slider). Both screenshots below were captured the same day. Growth-band figures for GetResponse are from the 29 October 2026 verification, cross-checked against independent pricing trackers updated in October 2026, because GetResponse's page shows prices in rupees to Indian visitors (see the note below). Payment options come from GetResponse's official payment help page. Prices are in US dollars unless stated otherwise, and GetResponse's prices are net amounts — local taxes may be added at checkout.

One India-specific observation from today's check: if you open GetResponse's pricing page from India, it displays prices in rupees. At 1,000 contacts on yearly billing it showed Starter at ₹1,479.43 a month, Marketer at ₹4,594.03 and Creator at ₹5,372.68 — the dollar annual rates converted, not a separate Indian price list. So use the dollar figures below for comparison, and expect your card to be charged the converted amount plus any applicable taxes.

## The free plan: Kit is far ahead, with one catch

**Kit's free plan** is one of the most generous free tiers in email marketing:

- Free up to 10,000 subscribers (only unique, active subscribers count)
- Unlimited landing pages, unlimited forms, unlimited broadcasts
- Audience tagging and segmentation
- Sell digital products and subscriptions (3.5% + 30c fee per sale)
- Newsletter feed and website

**GetResponse's free account** looks small next to it:

- 500 contacts and 2,500 newsletters a month (per independent reviews; the pricing page itself currently advertises only a 14-day free trial, with no free-plan card, so treat the 500-contact figure as reported, not as something the pricing page shows)
- 14 days of premium features first, then the limits apply
- GetResponse branding on every message (removed on paid plans)
- Landing page visits capped at 1,000 unique visitors a month
- Webinars limited to 10 attendees during the trial

But here is the twist in the other direction: **GetResponse's free account lets you build workflows** (without dynamic segment filters), while **Kit's free plan has no visual automation builder** on today's official comparison table — automation starts with Creator. Some older guides mention one automation on Kit's free plan, but that does not match the current page. So "free automation" favours GetResponse's free account, while "free list size" favours Kit by a huge margin.

## Automation: the two tools gate it differently

Automation is the feature that confuses beginners most, because the two tools gate it in opposite ways:

- **GetResponse Starter ($19/mo): exactly 1 custom automation workflow.** Run a welcome series and you have used it up; a second sequence means upgrading to Marketer ($59/mo) for unlimited workflows.
- **Kit Creator ($39/mo): unlimited visual automations and unlimited sequences.** The free plan has no automation builder at all, but once you are on Creator there is no count limit.
- **Kit Creator Pro ($79/mo):** adds advanced A/B testing (up to 5 subject lines), engagement scoring, deliverability reporting and the newsletter referral system.

The practical result: if you need two or three automated sequences (welcome + launch + re-engagement), GetResponse costs you $59 a month while Kit costs $39. But Kit gives you nothing automated for free, while GetResponse's free account gives you basic workflows.

## Landing pages, forms and selling: who gives what

**Landing pages and forms:** Both offer unlimited landing pages and forms (Kit free included; GetResponse paid plans unlimited, free account visit-capped). Kit's edge is that its newsletter feed and website come free too — a decent home for your newsletter without a separate website.

**Selling digital products:** Kit lets you sell from the free plan upward — digital products, paid newsletters and subscriptions at 3.5% + 30c per sale, of which Kit itself takes only 0.6%. GetResponse's selling tools (sales funnels, promo codes) start at Marketer, and courses and webinars sit in Creator.

**Webinars and courses:** GetResponse is the clear winner here. Creator ($69/mo) bundles webinars (up to 100 attendees), a course creator (up to 500 students) and paid newsletters in one dashboard — saving the cost of a separate webinar tool. Kit has no native webinar hosting, so webinars mean bolting on another tool.

## Paying from India: a card is essential, UPI is not confirmed

This is the most practical section for Indian beginners:

- **GetResponse:** The official payment help page lists Visa, MasterCard, Discover, Maestro, JCB and American Express cards, plus Apple Pay and Google Pay. **In India, PayPal and PayU are also accepted.** Your card must have recurring and international transactions enabled — GetResponse warns about this itself.
- **Kit:** Plans are paid by card through Stripe. You need an international-transaction-enabled card.
- **UPI:** UPI appears on neither GetResponse's official payment list nor as a confirmed option for Kit. If a third-party site claims UPI support, do not treat that as official confirmation. The safe route is an international card, or PayPal/PayU for GetResponse.

One more India-specific point: both tools bill in US dollars. Your card's exchange rate and bank forex markup can push the final cost above the dollar figure — so before committing to an annual plan, check what one month actually costs on your statement.

## How the cost grows with your list

List growth is where the two pricing models behave very differently:

| List size | GetResponse Starter | GetResponse Marketer | GetResponse Creator | Kit Creator (monthly) | Kit Pro (monthly) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1,000 | $19/mo | $59/mo | $69/mo | $39/mo | $79/mo |
| 5,000 | $54/mo | $95/mo | $109/mo | $89/mo | $139/mo |
| 10,000 | $79/mo | $114/mo | $134/mo | $139/mo | $189/mo |

Two things to watch:

1. **Kit's $0 to $139 jump:** The free plan ends at 10,000 subscribers and you land directly on the $139-a-month Creator band. There is no gradual ramp. Kit's own page confirms the band prices — $89 at 5,000, $139 at 10,000 — and its billing FAQ says it auto-upgrades you when you cross a limit, while downgrades need a manual request.
2. **GetResponse's gap narrows:** At 1,000 contacts, Marketer costs about three times Starter; at 10,000 it costs only about 44% more. A large list makes Marketer easier to justify.

On annual billing, Kit charges ten months' price for twelve (Creator $390 a year at 1,000 subscribers), and GetResponse gives 18% off. But only buy annual once you are sure about the tool — GetResponse's annual plans are non-refundable if cancelled mid-year, and Kit's refund window is 30 days from signup. Test monthly first.

Also note Kit's Pro first-year offer, live on the pricing page today: an extra 25% off the first year of Pro ($50 a month instead of $66 at 1,000 subscribers, $87 instead of $116 at 5,000, $119 instead of $158 at 10,000). The page states clearly that the discount applies only to the first year and the plan renews at the standard rate after that — so price year two, not just year one.

## Which one for whom: the straight decision guide

**Choose Kit if:**
- You want to start free and build a list up to 10,000 subscribers without paying
- You are a creator — writer, YouTuber, coach, newsletter publisher
- You want to sell digital products or a paid newsletter without a paid plan
- You like tag-based segmentation

**Choose GetResponse if:**
- You need automation, webinars and courses in one tool from day one
- You run ecommerce or sales funnels (abandoned cart, promo codes)
- You want to pay from India via PayPal or PayU
- Your list is already large and you want unlimited sends (all paid plans include unlimited monthly sends)

**Choose neither if:** You only send an occasional email and need no automation at all — a simple free newsletter tool will do, and you will save the money.
