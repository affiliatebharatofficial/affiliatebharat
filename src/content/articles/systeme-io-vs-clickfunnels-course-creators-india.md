## India angle: UPI, INR aur payment gateways — sabse bada farak

Ye section Indian course creators ke liye sabse important hai, kyunki yahan dono tools me asli farak hai.

**Systeme.io + Razorpay = UPI milta hai.** Systeme.io ke supported payment gateways me Razorpay hai. Razorpay checkout par aapke Indian students **UPI, netbanking, cards aur wallets se INR me pay** kar sakte hain. Systeme.io ki help pages ke mutabiq INR support karta hai, kuch limits ke saath — jaise PayPal par INR sirf domestic transactions ke liye hai, aur Stripe par American Express INR transactions me supported nahi hai. Razorpay ki apni fees (lagbhag 2% + GST, third-party reported) alag se lagegi, lekin aapka student jis tareeke se pay karna chahta hai, woh tareeka mil jayega. India me UPI digital payments ka 80% se zyada hissa hai — UPI na hona matlab sales khona hai.

**ClickFunnels par Razorpay/Instamojo nahi hai.** ClickFunnels ki official support page (March 2026) saaf kehti hai: *"Currently, we don't integrate with RazorPay and Instamojo."* Course payments ClickFunnels ke Payments AI ya Stripe ke through hote hain — card, Apple Pay, PayPal. Aur Stripe India UPI, netbanking ya BNPL support nahi karta (Stripe ki apni support page ke mutabiq). Matlab aapka Indian student UPI se course nahi khareed sakta — use international card ya PayPal chahiye hoga. Bahut se Indian buyers ke paas PayPal nahi hota, aur international transactions on kiya hua card bhi sabke paas nahi hota.

**Subscription ki payment bhi dollar me.** Dono tools ki subscription USD me bill hoti hai — na Systeme.io, na ClickFunnels UPI/RuPay se apni subscription leta hai. International transactions on kiya hua Visa/MasterCard credit ya debit card chahiye hoga, aur bank conversion + forex markup ke baad keemat checkout wale figure se thodi zyada padegi. Farq sirf itna hai ki Systeme.io par $17/mo ka matlab lagbhag ₹1,500/mo hai, aur ClickFunnels par $97/mo ka matlab lagbhag ₹8,500/mo — forex markup dono par lagega, lekin chhoti rakam par chhota asar hoga.

## Kaunsa tool kis ke liye? Decision guide

- **Pehla course, budget zero ya kam:** Systeme.io Free plan. Bina card ke shuru karein, 1 course aur 500 students tak bechein, phir $17/mo par jayein. Koi risk nahi.
- **Course chal raha hai, webinars se bechte hain:** Systeme.io Webinar $47/mo. 20 courses, 10,000 students, 10 evergreen webinars — ClickFunnels ke $197/mo wale Scale plan se zyada courses, chauthai keemat par.
- **Indian audience, UPI zaroori hai:** Systeme.io + Razorpay. Ye combination hi UPI checkout deta hai. ClickFunnels par ye option hi nahi hai.
- **Badi team, agency, ya multiple clients:** ClickFunnels Scale ($197/mo) ya Optimize ($297/mo). 5–10 workspaces aur team members, 75K–150K contacts — ye team features Systeme.io ke Custom plan (quote par) me ja kar milte hain.
- **Funnel templates aur community chahiye:** ClickFunnels ki community aur template library badi hai — agar aapko ready-made funnel templates aur "funnel hacking" training chahiye, to woh uski taakat hai. Lekin uske liye $97/mo entry fee deni hogi.
- **Sab kuch unlimited chahiye:** Systeme.io Unlimited $97/mo — wahi keemat jo ClickFunnels ka entry plan hai, lekin sab kuch unlimited.

## Aakhri baat

Systeme.io aur ClickFunnels dono achhe tools hain — lekin woh alag logo ke liye bane hain. ClickFunnels ek mature funnel platform hai jo badi teams aur scale par chalne wale businesses ke liye bana hai, aur uski keemat bhi waisi hi hai. Systeme.io ek solo creator ka tool hai: free se shuru karo, $17/mo par badho, aur Indian students se UPI me payment lo.

Agar aapka pehla course hai, to $97/mo kharch karke shuru karna samajhdari nahi hai — pehle Systeme.io ke free plan par course validate karein, pehli sales aane par $17/mo par jayein. Jab aapki team badhe aur sales scale par hon, tab ClickFunnels par vichar karein — tab tak aapko pata hoga ki aapko uske advanced features ki zaroorat hai ya nahi. Aur jo bhi tool lein, pehle uska free plan ya trial lekar apna course ka ek funnel bana kar dekh lein — 14 din ka trial, ek course ka test, aur aapko khud pata chal jayega ki kaunsa tool aapke kaam ka hai.
