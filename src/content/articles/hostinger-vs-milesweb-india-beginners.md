Farak teen jagah hai — **renewal price, kitni websites milti hain, aur support kis bhasha me milta hai**. Aur ek aur farak hai jo pehli nazar me dikhta hi nahi: MilesWeb ka "same price at renewal" wala purana claim ab khatm ho chuka hai, aur uska plan lineup bhi badal gaya hai. Is article me hum dono ki aaj, **26 November 2026 ko live-verified prices** compare karenge — intro price se lekar 4 saal ke total kharche tak — taaki aap pehla plan galat na chunein.

Ye analysis public specs aur official pricing pages par based hai — koi fake "humne khud test kiya" claim nahi. Dono sites ki prices aaj subah live verify ki gayi hain.

## Beginners Ke Liye Kya-Kya Matter Karta Hai

Price ke alawa, pehli website banate waqt ye cheezein matter karti hain:

### Hindi support — MilesWeb saaf aage

MilesWeb apne page par saaf likhta hai: **"24/7 support in English & Hindi"**. Hostinger ki official India site par Hindi support ka koi confirmed statement nahi milta — sirf "multilingual 24/7 support" likha hai. Jab site down ho aur aapko English me technical ticket likhni pade, tab Hindi support luxury nahi, zaroorat ban jati hai. Is mamle me MilesWeb verified option hai. Humare [HostGator vs MilesWeb Hindi support wale article](/hostgator-vs-milesweb-hindi-support/) me bhi yehi nikla tha — MilesWeb ekmatra host hai jo Hindi support ko khulkar likhta hai.

### Setup aur control panel — dono beginner-friendly

Dono par 1-click WordPress install, free SSL, AI website builder aur free migration milti hai. Hostinger ka **hPanel** apna custom panel hai — clean aur aasan; MilesWeb **mPanel** deta hai, jo cPanel jaisa hai. Jo log cPanel se aaye hain unko mPanel familiar lagega, jo bilkul naye hain unko hPanel thoda zyada simple lagega. Dono par migration free hai — Hostinger automatic tool + team help se karta hai, MilesWeb ki team khud move karti hai aur site tab tak Hostinger par live rehti hai jab tak test na ho jaye.

### Payment aur GST invoice — dono UPI lete hain

MilesWeb apne page par saaf likhta hai: rupees me **UPI, NetBanking ya card** se pay karo, aur business billing ke liye **GST invoice** milta hai. Hostinger India checkout par bhi UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), NetBanking aur RuPay accept karta hai — ye multiple November 2026 reviews aur Hostinger ki payment-methods help page se corroborated hai. Agar aap business ke liye hosting le rahe ho aur GST input credit chahiye to dono par invoice milta hai — MilesWeb par ye page par likha hai, Hostinger par checkout par confirm kar lein.

### Data centre — dono India me

MilesWeb apne page par **India Data Center** list karta hai. Hostinger ke baare me independent November 2026 reviews ke mutabiq Mumbai me Tier-3 data centre hai jo setup ke waqt select kiya ja sakta hai — ye official pricing page par nahi likha, isliye isko third-party reading ke roop me lein. Indian visitors ke liye India data centre ka matlab hai kam latency — site tez khulti hai.
