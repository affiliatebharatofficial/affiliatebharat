## Free SSL vs paid SSL: kab extra paise dene chahiye

Free SSL aam taur par Domain Validated (DV) certificate hota hai. Ye verify karta hai ki domain aapka hai, aur browser me padlock + HTTPS deta hai. Blog, portfolio, business site aur chhote online store ke liye ye kaafi hai — visitors ko farq nahi padta ki certificate free hai ya paid, unhe sirf padlock dikhta hai.

Paid SSL tab sochna chahiye jab:

- **Wildcard chahiye:** ek certificate se `blog.example.com`, `shop.example.com` jaise unlimited subdomains secure karne hain.
- **Multi-domain chahiye:** ek certificate se kai alag domains secure karne hain.
- **Organization Validation chahiye:** company ka naam certificate me dikhana hai (bade brands aur financial sites ke liye).

MilesWeb ke apne blog ke mutabiq uske paid SSL prices ye hain: Single Domain DV SSL ₹799/saal, Multi-domain SAN SSL ₹2,999/saal, Wildcard DV SSL ₹5,999/saal. Ye figures sirf ye batane ke liye hain ki paid SSL ka kharcha kitna hota hai — aam blog ke liye ye kharcha zaroori nahi hai. Aur ek baat saaf rakhein: **paid SSL lene se website fast ya Google me upar nahi aati** — ye sirf certificate ka type badalta hai, security ka basic level free SSL me bhi wahi hai.

## Free SSL milne ke baad bhi ye 4 cheezein check karein

SSL included hona aur SSL sahi se chalna — ye do alag baatein hain. Hosting khareedne ke baad ye checklist chalayein:

1. **HTTPS redirect on karein.** Hosting panel ya WordPress settings me force-HTTPS enable karein, taaki `http://` kholne par bhi visitor `https://` par pahunche. Bina redirect ke dono versions chalenge aur Google duplicate samajh sakta hai.
2. **Auto-renewal confirm karein.** Free SSL aam taur par 90 din ka hota hai aur auto-renew hota hai. Panel me certificate ki expiry date dekh lein — agar auto-renew fail hua to site par warning aa jayegi.
3. **Mixed content fix karein.** Agar page HTTPS par hai lekin koi image ya script `http://` se load ho rahi hai, to browser padlock ke saath warning dikhayega. WordPress me "Really Simple SSL" jaisa plugin ya search-replace se ye fix hota hai.
4. **Padlock khud check karein.** Site live hone ke baad browser me khud kholkar dekhein — padlock dikh raha hai ya "Not Secure"? Ye 30 second ka test hosting company ke daave se zyada bharosemand hai.
