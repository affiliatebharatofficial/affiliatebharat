## Kharidne se pehle Hindi support ka 2-minute test

Official claim par aankh band karke bharosa mat karo — chahe koi bhi host ho. Ye test kharidne se pehle karo:

1. **Live chat kholo** aur Hindi me likho: "Mujhe WordPress blog ke liye hosting chahiye, kaunsa plan sahi rahega?"
2. **Dekho jawab kis language me aata hai** — poora Hindi, Hinglish, ya English me "please write in English" jaisa jawab?
3. **Ek technical sawaal poochho** — jaise "SSL free hai ya alag se charge hai?" — kyunki sales jawab aur technical jawab ki quality alag ho sakti hai.
4. **Timing note karo** — raat ko 11 baje bhi chat kholkar dekho, kyunki "24/7" ka asli test off-hours me hi hota hai.

Ye test free hai, aur iska result kisi bhi review article se zyada bharosemand hai — kyunki ye aapka apna experience hoga, kisi aur ka claim nahi.
