## Semrush Free Trial: 7 Din, Card Zaroori, Auto-Bill

Semrush ka standard trial **7 din** ka hota hai. Ye SEO, Starter aur Pro+ — teeno plans par milta hai; Advanced plan par trial nahi, sirf sales demo milta hai. Trial me aapko us plan ki poori limits milti hain — jaise SEO plan par 5 websites monitor karna aur roz 500 keywords track karna.

Lekin trial ke chaar rules hain jo log aksar miss kar dete hain:

1. **Credit card zaroori hai.** Semrush ke apne blog aur help center ke mutabiq trial shuru karne ke liye card details dene padte hain. Sirf free account card-free hai. Semrush ke mutabiq trial ke liye **physical card** chahiye — virtual card accept nahi hota.
2. **Auto-bill hota hai.** Agar 7 din ke andar cancel na kiya to trial apne aap paid subscription me badal jata hai — jo plan aur billing period aapne signup ke waqt chuna tha, usi rate par.
3. **Export band hai.** Trial ke dauraan data export disable rehta hai — aap sab kuch dekh sakte ho, lekin reports download nahi kar sakte. Apna analysis trial ke andar hi khatam karo.
4. **Ek baar hi milta hai.** Trial ek account par sirf ek baar use ho sakta hai. Kuch partner links se 14-day trial ka daava milta hai, lekin standard on-site offer 7 din ka hi hai — purana 30-day trial 2023 me band ho gaya tha.

**Cancel kaise karein:** Semrush ki cancellation page par jaakar cancel karo, aur jo confirmation link email par aata hai us par click karna mat bhoolo — bina us click ke cancellation poori nahi hoti. Teesri-party guides ke mutabiq unpaid trial ki cancellation ko effect hone me 3 business days tak lag sakte hain, isliye trial ke aakhri din ka wait mat karo — **5th ya 6th din tak cancel kar do.**

### Trial kab lena chahiye?

Trial tabhi lo jab ek hafte ka SEO kaam pehle se ready ho: keyword list taiyaar ho, competitor sites ki list ho, aur site audit karwana ho. Khali trial shuru karke 3 din sochne me bita diye to 7 din yun hi nikal jayenge — aur 8th din card se $139.95 kat jayenge.

### Semrush for Education — lekin India ke liye nahi

Semrush ka ek official program hai: **Semrush for Education.** Ye 3 mahine ke liye free access deta hai, Semrush Academy courses aur certificate ke saath. 350+ universities aur 25,000+ students isme shaamil hain.

Lekin iski teen shartein hain jo Bharat ke students ke liye mushkil banati hain:

1. **Individual student apply nahi kar sakta.** Sirf professor ya department head apne college ke liye partnership apply karta hai.
2. **Eligible countries ki list me India nahi hai.** List me US, Canada, UK, Australia, New Zealand aur Europe hain.
3. **Sirf 3 mahine ke liye hai.** Uske baad access khatam.

Toh agar aap Bharat ke student hain, to Semrush for Education aapke liye band raasta hai. Apne college ke professor se baat karke pooch sakte ho — koi nuksaan nahi — lekin is par bharosa mat rakho.

### Students ke liye asli plan

1. **Free account + Google Search Console** — ₹0 me shuru karo. GSC apni site ka asli performance data deta hai (kis keyword par rank ho rahe ho, kaunse pages par clicks aa rahe hain), aur Semrush free account roz 10 competitor/keyword lookups deta hai.
2. **Semrush Academy ke free courses** — tool khareede bina bhi Academy ke free on-demand courses aur certificate mil sakte hain. Ye CV me kaam aate hain.
3. **Trial tabhi lo jab kaam ready ho** — ek hafte ka SEO kaam pehle se plan karke, aur cancel ka reminder lagakar.
4. **Budget alternative: Ubersuggest** — agar ₹0 stack se kaam na chale to Ubersuggest Individual dekho: $29/mo monthly, $192/saal ($16/mo) annual, ya $290 ek baar lifetime. Free tier roz 3 searches deta hai, aur Chrome extension se roz lagbhag 40 overlay searches milte hain. Ye figures multiple Nov 2026 readings se corroborated hain.
