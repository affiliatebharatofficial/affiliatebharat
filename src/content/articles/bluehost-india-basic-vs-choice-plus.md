## Seedhi baat pehle: Basic aur Choice Plus ab nahi milte

Agar aap Google par "bluehost india basic vs choice plus which plan" search karke yahan aaye hain, to sabse pehle ek zaroori sach: **ye dono plans ab Bluehost India par bechne ke liye nahi dikhte.**

27 October 2026 ko Bluehost ki official site live check ki gayi to ye mila:

- Purani URL `bluehost.in/hosting/shared-hosting` ab **404** deti hai.
- `bluehost.in` ab `bluehost.com/in` par redirect hota hai.
- Shared hosting page par ab **Starter, Business aur eCommerce Essentials** plans hain — Basic, Plus ya Choice Plus naam ka koi plan card nahi hai.
- Ek hi jagah purana naam dikhta hai: "Domain Manager" section me ek line me "Plus and Business Pro accounts" ka zikr — ye koi khareedne wala plan nahi hai.

Iska matlab ye article ka sawaal badal gaya hai. Ab sawaal ye nahi hai ki "Basic lein ya Choice Plus", balki ye hai ki **"Basic lene wale ko ab kaunsa plan lena chahiye, aur Choice Plus lene wale ko kaunsa?"** Isi sawaal ka jawab is article me hai — live-verified prices ke saath.

Ye analysis public specs aur official pricing data ke basis par hai. Humne Bluehost ke plans khud khareedkar test nahi kiye hain, isliye yahan koi "maine test kiya" wala daava nahi milega — sirf official page par jo likha hai, wahi.

## Purane plans vs naye plans: mapping table

Pehle ye samajh lein ki purane plans kya the. Neeche purane figures 9 October 2026 ko Bluehost ki official help page se verified the — aaj wo plans nahi bikte, isliye ye sirf mapping ke liye hain:

Aur aaj ke naye plans (27 October 2026, official page se live):

| Naya plan | Intro price (36-mo) | Renewal | Websites | Storage |

Mapping seedhi hai:

- **Basic → Starter.** Basic lene wala reader ek website wala blogger tha; Starter uska replacement hai — balki websites ke mamle me behtar hai (1 ki jagah 10), lekin storage kam hai (50 GB ki jagah 10 GB NVMe).
- **Choice Plus → Business.** Choice Plus lene wala multi-site user tha; Business uska kareebi replacement hai — 50 websites, domain privacy pehle saal free, weekly backups aur AI malware protection ke saath.

Ek farq note karein: purane plans ke renewal prices official site par **kabhi publish nahi hue** the. Naye plans me ye sudhaar hai — renewal price plan card par hi likha hai. Isliye aaj ka buyer pehle se jaan sakta hai ki 3 saal baad bill kitna badhega.

## Har naye plan ka detail: kaunsa kiske liye

### Starter — ₹383/mo (renews ₹960/mo): pehle blog ke liye

Starter card par likha hai: "Perfect for your first website or blog." Isme milta hai:

- 10 websites, 10 GB NVMe SSD storage, 40,000 visits/mo tak ideal
- Free domain pehle saal, free Let's Encrypt SSL, weekly website backups
- Free CDN, managed WordPress updates, 99.99% uptime SLA, 30-day money-back guarantee
- **Domain privacy Starter me listed nahi hai** — ye ek kami hai jo Business me poori hoti hai

**Kiske liye:** Ek ya do blogs, portfolio site, ya chhoti business site. 10 GB storage ka matlab hai: text-heavy blog ke liye kaafi, lekin agar aapki site par bhaari images ya videos hain to jaldi bhar sakta hai.

**3 saal ka total:** ₹383 × 36 = ₹13,788 intro term par. Renewal par ₹960/mo — yani 2.5 guna jump. Ye jump dekhkar hi decide karein ki 36-month term lena hai ya nahi.

### Business — ₹672/mo (renews ₹1,345/mo): multi-site users ke liye

Business card par "MOST POPULAR" ka tag hai. Isme milta hai:

- 50 websites, 50 GB NVMe SSD storage, 200,000 visits/mo tak ideal
- Domain privacy **pehle saal free**, weekly backups
- AI-powered malware detection & removal, web application firewall, DDoS protection
- Free domain pehle saal, free SSL, 30-day money-back guarantee

**Kiske liye:** Jo blogger ek se zyada sites chalata hai, client sites manage karta hai, ya domain privacy (WHOIS me apna naam-number chhupana) chahta hai. Choice Plus ke purane users ke liye yehi natural upgrade path hai — haan, "unlimited websites" ki jagah ab 50 websites ki limit hai, lekin 50 sites ek aam user ke liye practically kaafi hain.

**3 saal ka total:** ₹672 × 36 = ₹24,192 intro term par. Renewal ₹1,345/mo — 2 guna jump.

### eCommerce Essentials — ₹1,441/mo (renews ₹2,113/mo): online store ke liye

- 100 websites, 100 GB NVMe storage, 400,000 visits/mo tak ideal
- Business wali saari security features + WooCommerce tools: secure payment processing, WooCommerce auto-install, product subscriptions, visitor memberships, paid courses, affiliate program
- Domain privacy pehle saal free, weekly backups

**Kiske liye:** Sirf tab jab aapko sach me online store chalana hai — courses bechne hain, memberships deni hain ya subscriptions manage karni hain. Sirf blog ke liye ye plan overkill hai; us paise me Business + alag tools saste padenge.

## Site-wide sale: kya sach hai, kya dhyaan rakhna hai

Bluehost ke homepage par abhi "SITE-WIDE SALE / OUR BEST DEALS YET" ka banner chal raha hai. Terms dialog ke mutabiq ye offer **1 October se 30 November 2026, 11:59 PM ET tak** valid hai, aur isme bundle discount hai: ek eligible product par 15% off, do par 20% aur teen ya zyada par 25% — jo checkout par automatically apply hota hai, aur sirf pehle 12-month term par.

Yahan do honest notes hain:

1. **Discount ki kahani me farq hai.** Page ka title "Save 70%" kehta hai, lekin Standard plan cards par 60% (Starter), 50% (Business) aur 32% (eCommerce Essentials) ke badge hain. Humne card wale figures hi table me likhe hain, kyunki wahi aapko khareedte waqt dikhenge.
2. **Bundle discount card prices par stack hoga ya nahi, ye page par saaf nahi likha.** Isliye checkout par final amount dekhna hi ekmatra bharosemand tareeqa hai — kisi aggregator ke "extra 10% off" wale daave par bharosa mat karein.

Ek aur baat: terms me "taxes and fees extra" likha hai, lekin **GST ke baare me pricing page par koi statement nahi mila**. Isliye hum ye nahi keh sakte ki upar wali prices me GST included hai ya extra lagega. Checkout page par tax line zaroor check karein — aur agar aapko GST invoice chahiye business ke liye, to support se pehle hi confirm kar lein. ## Kaunsa plan lein: decision guide

- **Sirf ek blog shuru karna hai, budget tight hai → Starter.** Lekin 10 GB storage ka dhyaan rakhein; images ko compress karke upload karein.
- **2–10 sites, ya domain privacy chahiye → Business.** Ye aaj ka sabse balanced plan hai, aur Choice Plus ka asli successor bhi.
- **Online store, courses ya memberships bechni hain → eCommerce Essentials.** Sirf blog ke liye mat lein.
- **Site pehle se heavy hai, CPU chahiye → High Performance tab ka Pro.** Usse upar tabhi jayein jab traffic sach me zyada ho.

Aur ek practical salah: **36-month term lene se pehle renewal price ka hisaab laga lein.** Starter 3 saal baad ₹960/mo ho jaata hai — agar aapko lagta hai ki 3 saal me aap host badal sakte hain ya kam price wale plan par ja sakte hain, to chhota term bhi soch sakte hain (lekin chhote term par monthly price zyada hoga, ye checkout par dikhega).

Agar aap abhi blog shuru karne ka poora kharcha samajhna chahte hain, to [WordPress blog start karne me kitna kharcha aata hai](/wordpress-blog-start-kitna-kharcha/) padhein — usme domain + hosting + theme ka poora budget breakdown hai.
