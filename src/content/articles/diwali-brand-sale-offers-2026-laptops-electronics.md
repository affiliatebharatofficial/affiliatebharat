## Abhi khareedun ya wait karun? — Decision framework

**ABHI KHARIDO (brand store se) agar:**

- Tumhara shortlisted model Asus/Lenovo store par achhe effective price me hai — ye sales **18–20 October tak khatm** ho rahi hain, Diwali waves tak wapas nahi aayengi
- Tum student ho aur Lenovo ka 10% student discount use kar sakte ho — marketplace par ye benefit nahi milta
- Tumhe official warranty + exchange bonus chahiye — brand stores ka exchange process sabse smooth hota hai

**WAIT KARO agar:**

- Tumhara model flexible hai aur tum **bank offers (10% instant discount)** par sabse zyada bharosa karte ho — Amazon GIF par SBI 10% offer **abhi live hai**, Flipkart BBD me aakhri ghanto me; brand-store price ko aaj hi live marketplace price se compare karo
- Tum [Flipkart vs Amazon Diwali sale comparison](/en/big-billion-days-vs-great-indian-festival-2026/) me bataye gaye bank-offer stacking rules samajh chuke ho

**Dhyan rahe:** "25 October (Amazon) / 29 October (Flipkart) Diwali wave" ki dates **sirf aggregator speculation hain** — Amazon GIF to 8 October se live hi hai, aur koi alag future Diwali phase officially announce nahi hua. Wait karne ka matlab hai kisi unconfirmed wave ka intezaar, jabki dono marketplaces par deals abhi live hain.

## Bank offers, EMI aur GST: checkout se pehle ki checklist

1. **Bank discount checkout par verify karo:** Asus Zenbook A14 ka ₹11,000 bank discount listing par dikhta hai — apne card par applicable hai ya nahi, ye cart me confirm karo.
2. **No-Cost EMI ka tenure check karo:** Lenovo 12 months tak NCEMI de raha hai, Asus 24 months Low-Cost EMI — "No-Cost" ka matlab processing fee zero nahi hota, fee check karo.
3. **GST invoice mat bhoolo:** Brand stores India me GST invoice deti hain. Freelancer/creator ho to **checkout par GSTIN add karo** — 18% GST ka input tax credit tumhare business ke liye real savings hai. Marketplace par seller ka GST invoice order se pehle verify karo.
4. **Exchange bonus me purane laptop ki valuation check karo:** Lenovo ka ₹15K tak exchange bonus "up to" hai — tumhare purane device ki condition par actual value depend karegi.
5. **Prices ghanto me badal sakti hain:** Ye saari offers **18 October 2026 ko reported/verified** hain. Checkout par final price hi asli deal hai — banner ki percentage nahi.
