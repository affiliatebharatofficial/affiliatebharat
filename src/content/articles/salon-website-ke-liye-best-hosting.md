## Salon website ko asal me kya chahiye

Ek salon ya parlour ki website kisi blog ya news site jaisi nahi hoti. Yahan visitors article padhne nahi, **customer banne** aate hain. Isliye hosting choose karne se pehle ye samajh lo ki site par kya-kya hoga:

- **Booking / enquiry system:** WhatsApp button ya simple booking form — taaki customer turant appointment book kar sake. Ye sabse important feature hai.
- **Photo gallery:** Haircut, bridal makeup, facial results — salon ka kaam visual hai. 30–50 high-quality photos aam baat hai, isliye thodi storage chahiye.
- **Google Maps embed:** "Near me" searches se aane wale customers ke liye location sabse bada factor hai. Map fast load hona chahiye.
- **Fast mobile load:** 80%+ customers phone se aayenge. 3 second se zyada load time hua to customer wapas Google par chala jayega — aur pados wale salon ki site khol lega.
- **SSL certificate:** Booking form me customer apna naam aur phone number dega — bina SSL (https) ke browser "Not Secure" warning dikhayega. Dono recommended hosts me free SSL milta hai.

Ye sab cheezen shared hosting par aaram se chalti hain. Aapko mahange VPS ya "business cloud" ki zaroorat nahi hai — wo paise marketing par lagana zyada samajhdari hogi.

## Ek salon site par kitna traffic aata hai

Realistic expectations rakhna zaroori hai taaki hosting over-buy na karo:

- **Naya salon (pehle 6 mahine):** 200–800 visitors/month — zyadatar Google Maps aur "salon near me" searches se.
- **Jam hua local salon:** 1,000–5,000 visitors/month — repeat customers + Instagram/Facebook se traffic.
- **Multiple branches / bridal specialist:** 5,000–20,000 visitors/month — tab bhi shared hosting handle kar lega.

Shared hosting aam taur par mahine ke **10,000–25,000 visits** tak aaram se sambhal leta hai. Matlab 95% salon websites ke liye shared hosting hi sahi answer hai. "Unlimited" ya cloud plan lene ka matlab sirf tab hai jab aapko pehle se pata ho ki heavy traffic aayega.

## Hosting kharidte time in 5 baaton ka dhyan rakhein

Plan select karne se pehle ye checklist ek baar zaroor padh lein — ye wohi points hain jahan pehli baar website banane wale aksar phas jaate hain:

1. **Billing term samajh lo.** ₹149/mo wali price tabhi milti hai jab aap 48-month term ek saath lete ho — matlab pehle din ~₹7,150 ka payment. Agar monthly ya yearly billing chahiye to per-month rate zyada hoga. Apne cash flow ke hisaab se term chuno, sirf headline price dekhkar nahi.

2. **Money-back policy check karo.** MilesWeb 30-day money-back deta hai — matlab agar pehle mahine me laga ki service theek nahi hai, to paise wapas mil sakte hain. Kharidne se pehle refund terms padhna kabhi waste nahi jaata.

3. **GST ka hisaab rakho.** MilesWeb ki prices me 18% GST alag se judta hai — ₹199/mo ka plan asal me ~₹235/mo padta hai. Ye chhoti rakam hai lekin business budgeting me isko pehle se likh lo taaki hisaab gadbad na ho.

4. **Free domain ki expiry date note karo.** Dono hosts pehle saal domain free dete hain, lekin dusre saal se ~₹1,000–1,500/saal lagenge. Apne phone me renewal se 15 din pehle ka reminder lagao — expired domain wapas lena mehenga aur jhanjhat bhara kaam hai.

5. **Backup ka bharosa sirf host par mat rakho.** Host weekly ya daily backup deta hai, lekin mahine me ek baar khud bhi site ka backup download kar lo. Ye 5-minute ka kaam hai aur ek din bahut kaam aayega — khaas taur par jab aap site me bade changes kar rahe ho.

## DIY vs developer: khud banayein ya kisi se banwayein

Ye woh sawaal hai jo har salon owner poochta hai. Honest answer:

**Khud banayein (DIY) — jab:**
- Aapko basic computer chalana aata hai aur YouTube se seekhne ka patience hai
- Website simple chahiye: services, prices, gallery, WhatsApp button, map
- Budget ₹2,000–3,000/saal tak limited hai
- WordPress + free theme + WhatsApp chat plugin se ek decent salon site 1–2 din me ban jaati hai

**Developer se banwayein — jab:**
- Aapke paas time bilkul nahi hai aur business par focus karna hai
- Online advance payment wali proper booking system chahiye
- Ek baar me professional design chahiye jo Instagram feed jaisi lage

Ek beech ka rasta bhi hai: **pehle saal DIY** se shuru karo (kharcha sirf hosting ka), jab bookings badhne lagein tab developer ko upgrade ke liye hire karo. Isse aapko pata bhi chal jayega ki website se asal me kitne customers aa rahe hain — phir developer par kharcha karna justified lagega.

Aur haan — developer chahe jo bhi bole, hosting apne naam par khud kharido. Aise cases aam hain jahan developer apne account par hosting lekar gayab ho jaata hai aur site band ho jaati hai. Domain aur hosting ka control hamesha owner ke paas hona chahiye.
