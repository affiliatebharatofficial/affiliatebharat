## Transfer escape hatch + 1 November ki deadline

Coupon se bada hathiyar **transfer** hai. ICANN rules ke under:
- Domain **60+ din purana** hona chahiye (registration ya last transfer ke baad 60-day lock lagta hai).
- Transfer ki cost gaining registrar ke **one-year renewal price** jitni hoti hai — aur **1 saal registration me add** ho jata hai. Matlab Porkbun par transfer = $11.08 me 1 saal extra.
- Steps: GoDaddy account me domain unlock karo → WHOIS privacy hatao → EPP authorization code lo → Porkbun/Cloudflare par transfer initiate karo → email confirmation approve karo. 5–7 din lagte hain.

**Time-sensitive tip:** 1 November 2026 se .com ke wholesale prices badh rahe hain (Verisign price increase). Usse pehle multi-year renewal ya transfer karne se **aaj ke prices lock** ho jate hain un saalon ke liye. Agar renewal November ke aas-paas hai, to abhi action lo.
