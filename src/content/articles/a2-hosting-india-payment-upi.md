## Pehle ek confusion door karein: A2 Hosting ab Hosting.com hai

Agar aap purane articles padh rahe hain to wahan "A2 Hosting" naam milega — Startup, Drive aur Turbo plans ke saath. **April 2025 me A2 Hosting ne rebrand karke apna naam Hosting.com kar liya.** Isse pehle, January 2025 me World Host Group ne company ko acquire kiya tha. Purani a2hosting.com ab hosting.com par redirect hoti hai.

Iska matlab ye hai:

- **A2 Hosting India** naam ki koi alag Indian company ya Indian storefront nahi hai. Jo bhi site "A2 Hosting India" likh kar bech rahi hai, woh ya to purana naam use kar rahi hai, ya affiliate page hai.
- Aaj ke plans ke naam bhi badal gaye hain — ab shared hosting me **Starter, Pro aur Max** plans hain (5, 10 aur 50 websites), purane Startup/Drive naam nahi.
- Homepage par location selector "United States (Global)" dikhata hai — matlab aap global (US) storefront se khareed rahe hain, Indian storefront se nahi.

Ye rebrand isliye important hai kyunki UPI wala confusion isi se paida hua hai.

## Conflict: kuch blogs kehte hain "A2 par UPI chalta hai" — ye galat kyun hai

2. **Independent payment-page check, 6 November 2026.** Ek doosri site ne 6 November 2026 ko hosts ke official payment pages ko check kiya aur saaf likha: "Hosting.com (formerly A2 Hosting): No UPI — card, PayPal and bank transfer." Wahi check Hostinger, MilesWeb aur BigRock ke liye UPI ko "Yes" batata hai — matlab checker ne UPI ko pehchana, bas Hosting.com par woh mila hi nahi.
3. **Koi Indian storefront nahi hai.** UPI sirf wahi host de sakta hai jiska Indian payment gateway ho — jaise Hostinger India ya MilesWeb, jo rupees me bill karte hain. Hosting.com dollars me bill karta hai, US storefront se. Razorpay jaisa Indian gateway us checkout me kahin dikhta hi nahi.

## Agar UPI nahi chalta, to Hosting.com par kaise pay karein?

UPI na hone ka matlab ye nahi ki Indian buyer Hosting.com khareed hi nahi sakta. Ye raaste hain:

**1. International transactions on kiya hua card.** Visa ya MasterCard credit/debit card jisme international transactions on hon. Checkout dollars me hoga, aur aapka bank rupees me convert karega — conversion charges aur forex markup (aam taur par 3–3.5% ke aas-paas) alag se lag sakta hai. Card khareedne se pehle apne bank ki app me international transactions on kar lein, warna payment decline hogi.

**2. PayPal.** Hosting.com PayPal accept karta hai. PayPal account se, ya guest checkout se card ke through pay kar sakte hain. PayPal apna conversion rate lagata hai, jo bank se thoda mehenga ho sakta hai — checkout par final amount dekh kar hi pay karein.

**3. Skrill ya wire transfer.** Policy me dono likhe hain, lekin aam blogger ke liye ye practical nahi hain — Skrill par account aur fees ka jhanjhat hai, aur wire transfer me bank charges zyada hote hain.

**4. Cheque ya money order.** Policy me likhe hain, lekin India se ye tareeka bilkul practical nahi hai — is par waqt barbaad mat karein.

Ek aur zaroori baat: **Hosting.com ke saare plans aur domains auto-renew par set hote hain** — ye uski policy ke Section 2 me likha hai. Renewal se 3 din pehle payment try ki jati hai. Iska matlab aapka card renewal date tak valid aur international transactions ke liye on rehna chahiye, warna service suspend ho sakti hai. Renewal price ke baare me agle section me hai.

## Kaunsa host choose karein? Decision guide

- **Sirf UPI hai, international card nahi:** Hostinger ya MilesWeb. Dono par UPI, RuPay aur net banking chalti hai. Hostinger ka Premium ₹139/mo sabse balanced hai; MilesWeb ka Starter ₹69/mo sabse sasta entry point hai.
- **GST invoice chahiye (business ke liye):** MilesWeb ya Hostinger. MilesWeb apne homepage par GST invoice ka vaada karta hai; Hostinger bhi GST invoice deta hai.
- **Hosting.com ki speed chahiye (LiteSpeed + NVMe + Turbo):** Sirf tab jab aapke paas international card ya PayPal ho. UPI se pay karne ki koshish me checkout par atak jayenge, aur renewal bhi auto-charge nahi hoga.
- **Sabse sasta pehla saal:** Hosting.com ka Black Friday Starter $17.88/saal (lagbhag ₹1,580, bank conversion ke baad) — lekin doosre saal $167.88/saal yaad rakhein. MilesWeb Starter ₹69/mo + GST, 36 mahine ke liye ₹2,484 + GST, renewal ₹149/mo — 3 saal ka total zyada predictable hai.
- **Hindi support chahiye:** MilesWeb "Support in English or Hindi, 24/7" likhta hai; Hostinger ki support bhi Hindi me milti hai.

## UPI se pay karne ke practical steps

1. **Host chunein** — upar wale table se UPI wala host lein (Hostinger, MilesWeb ya BigRock).
2. **Plan aur term chunein** — lamba term (36 ya 48 mahine) sasta padta hai, lekin renewal price bhi check karein. Intro price sirf pehli term ke liye hota hai.
3. **Checkout par UPI select karein** — GPay, PhonePe, Paytm ya BHIM UPI app se approve karein. UPI se auto-renewal mandate hamesha kaam nahi karta — kuch banks UPI AutoPay support nahi karte, isliye renewal date calendar me note kar lein aur manual renew karein.
4. **GST invoice download karein** — business ke liye checkout ke baad invoice section se GST invoice le lein.
5. **International card se pay kar rahe hain to** — pehle bank app me international transactions on karein, aur checkout par dikhaya gaya USD amount aur bank ka conversion rate dono check karein. Forex markup ki wajah se final amount card par thoda zyada dikh sakta hai.
