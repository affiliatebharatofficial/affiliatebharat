## Mailchimp: sabse bada naam, sabse mehenga bill

Mailchimp email marketing ka sabse pehchana hua naam hai — templates, ecommerce integrations aur automation flows ke liye mashhoor. Lekin 10,000 contacts par uska bill chaaron me sabse zyada hai.

![Mailchimp ki pricing page 13 November 2026 — 5,001–10,000 contacts par Essentials ₹3,190/mo, Standard ₹4,180/mo, Premium ₹12,650/mo (pehle 12 mahine, 50% off); uske baad ₹6,380, ₹8,360 aur ₹25,300/mo](/screenshots/mailchimp-pricing-2026.png)
*Mailchimp ki pricing page 13 November 2026 ko — Indian visitors ko INR keematein dikhti hain, 5,001–10,000 contacts band*

Aaj ki live page par (Indian visitor, INR currency) 5,001–10,000 contacts wale band me:

- **Essentials — ₹3,190/mo pehle 12 mahine, phir ₹6,380/mo.** Basic automation aur scheduling milti hai, lekin multi-step automation nahi.
- **Standard — ₹4,180/mo pehle 12 mahine, phir ₹8,360/mo.** Multi-step automation, send-time optimization aur behavioural targeting milte hain. Monthly allowance: **120,000 emails/month** (contacts ka 12 guna).
- **Premium — ₹12,650/mo pehle 12 mahine, phir ₹25,300/mo.** Advanced segmentation, multivariate testing aur phone support — ye badi teams ke liye hai.

**Do baatein dhyaan dein.** Pehli: page par **"50% off for 12 months"** likha hai — purane guides me jo 15% off wali baat milti hai, woh aaj ki page par nahi dikhi (15% discount sirf nonprofits aur charities ke liye alag offer hai). Dusri: USD page par yehi Standard plan **$135/mo** bataya jata hai (October–November 2026 ki kai independent readings), jabki INR full price ₹8,360 lagbhag $95 ke barabar hai. Matlab aapko dikh rahi keemat is baat par depend karti hai ki page aapko kaunsi currency dikhata hai — checkout par final amount zaroor check karein.

Mailchimp ki billing ka ek aur pehlu hai jo bill badhata hai: woh **stored contacts** par charge karta hai — unsubscribed aur non-subscribed contacts bhi count hote hain jab tak aap unhe archive na karein, aur bill us mahine ke peak contact total par banta hai. Ek hi vyakti do audiences me ho to do baar count hota hai. Isliye list saaf rakhna yahan seedha paisa bachata hai.

**Saalaana kharcha (Standard):** full price par ₹8,360 × 12 = **₹1,00,320/saal**; pehle saal 50% off ke saath ₹50,160. USD hisab se $135 × 12 = $1,620/saal.

## Brevo: kam mail bhejo to sabse sasta, zyada bhejo to bill badhta hai

Brevo (pehle Sendinblue) ki billing hi alag hai — woh contacts par nahi, **bheje gaye emails par** charge karta hai. Isi liye kam frequency wale senders ke liye ye sabse sasta hai, aur zyada frequency wale ke liye mehenga ho jata hai.

![Brevo ki pricing page 13 November 2026 — Customize your plan calculator, Standard tab, 20,000 emails/month par ₹4,433.92/mo (yearly billing); 500,000 contacts included](/screenshots/brevo-pricing-2026.png)
*Brevo ki pricing page 13 November 2026 ko — Standard tab, 20,000 emails/month slider, yearly billing view*

Aaj ki live page par (Indian visitor, INR currency), "Customize your plan" calculator me 20,000 emails/month set karne par:

- **Starter — ₹2,505/mo monthly billing, ₹2,256.33/mo yearly billing (-10%).** 20,000 emails/month, basic automation.
- **Standard — ₹4,927/mo monthly billing, ₹4,433.92/mo yearly billing (-10%).** Unlimited automation, A/B testing, web tracking aur advanced features.
- **Free — ₹0, hamesha ke liye.** 300 emails/day ki limit ke saath.

**Yahan ek trap hai jo most buyers miss karte hain:** Brevo ke chhote email tiers par contact cap bhi hota hai — 5,000 emails wale tier me sirf 500 contacts, 10,000 emails par 1,500 contacts, 15,000 emails par 2,500 contacts. Aapki list 10,000 contacts ki hai, isliye 10,000 emails wala tier ($17/mo) aapke kaam ka nahi — aapko **20,000 emails wala tier** lena hi padega, jahan contact cap 500,000 ho jata hai. Matlab "mahine me ek mail = ₹2,505" hi aapka entry point hai, usse kam nahi.

USD figures (October–November 2026 readings): Starter 20,000 emails par $32/mo, Standard $69/mo; 40,000 emails par Starter $46/mo aur Standard ko 50,000-email tier ($97/mo) chahiye. INR figures phir se USD conversion se kam hain — Brevo bhi Indian visitors ko INR pricing dikhata hai.

**Saalaana kharcha:** Starter monthly billing par ₹2,505 × 12 = **₹30,060/saal** (yearly par ₹27,076); Standard monthly billing par ₹4,927 × 12 = **₹59,124/saal** (yearly par ₹53,207).

## GetResponse: unlimited sends wala beech ka rasta

GetResponse contacts par charge karta hai, lekin **har plan me unlimited monthly email sends** deta hai. Isi liye zyada mail bhejne walon ke liye ye Brevo se sasta padta hai.

Aaj 13 November 2026 ko GetResponse ki pricing page live browser me **teen baar timeout hui** — isliye is article me uska screenshot nahi hai. Lekin uski keematein kamzor nahi hain: official page ki November 2026 ki kai independent readings (3–12 November) me 10,000 contacts ki keematein ek jaise mili hain, aur 1,000-contact wali keematein aaj text fetch se confirm ki gayi hain.

- **Starter — $79/mo monthly billing, $64.78/mo yearly billing (-18%).** 10,000 contacts, unlimited sends, 1 automation workflow, landing page builder, AI tools.
- **Marketer — $114/mo monthly, $93.48/mo yearly.** Unlimited automation workflows, abandoned cart recovery, sales funnels.
- **Creator — $134/mo monthly, $109.88/mo yearly.** Webinars, course creator, paid newsletters — creators ke liye.
- **Free account + 14-day trial:** premium features 14 din tak bina card ke try kar sakte hain; uske baad free account ki limits lag jati hain.

Ek aur baat: agar aapki list apne tier se badh jaye (jaise forms ya integrations se naye subscribers aayein), to GetResponse **list extension fee** lagata hai — agle tier tak ka price difference, har mahine, jab tak extra contacts hatayein na jayein. Isliye tier ke bilkul kinare par list rakhna mehenga pad sakta hai.

**Saalaana kharcha (Starter):** monthly billing par $79 × 12 = **$948/saal** (lagbhag ₹83,400, ₹88/$1 ke hisab se — approximate); yearly billing par $64.78 × 12 = **$777.36/saal**. Note: 2 November 2026 ki ek reading me GetResponse Indian visitors ko INR keematein dikha raha tha (1,000 contacts par Starter ₹1,479.43/mo yearly), lekin 10,000 contacts ki INR keemat aaj verify nahi ho payi — checkout par check karein.

*Kit ki pricing page 13 November 2026 ko — yearly billing, 10,000 subscribers slider*

Aaj ki live page par (USD, Indian visitor ko bhi dollar me dikhata hai), 10,000 subscribers set karne par:

- **Pro — $189/mo monthly billing; yearly par $158/mo regular, pehle saal $119/mo ($1,418 first year, $850 ki bachat).** "EXTRA 25% OFF FIRST YEAR" limited-time offer chal raha hai — lekin page par iski koi end date nahi dikhi. Kit ka catch saaf hai: free plan list banane aur newsletter bhejne ke liye kaafi hai, lekin **pehli welcome sequence hi upgrade ka trigger** hai. 10,000 subscribers par free se Creator par jaana $0 se seedha $139/mo hai — beech me koi gradual ramp nahi. Aur digital products bechne par har plan me **3.5% + 30¢ per sale** ki commerce fee lagti hai (third-party reported).

**Saalaana kharcha (Creator):** monthly billing par $139 × 12 = **$1,668/saal**; yearly billing par **$1,390/saal**.

Kit ka bill sends se bilkul nahi badhta, lekin uski base keemat $139/mo hai — automation ke liye ye theek hai, sirf newsletter ke liye nahi.

## INR vs USD: aapko kaunsi keemat dikhegi?

Aaj ki live check me ek interesting farak dikha: **Mailchimp aur Brevo Indian visitors ko INR keematein dikhate hain, Kit USD dikhata hai.** Aur INR keematein dollar wali keemat ko ₹88 se multiply karne par jo aata hai, usse kam hain — Mailchimp Standard ka full price ₹8,360 hai jabki $135 × 88 = ₹11,880 hota; Brevo Standard ₹4,927 hai jabki $69 × 88 = ₹6,072 hota.

Iska matlab: purane guides me jo dollar figures milti hain, woh Indian reader ke liye zyada ho sakti hain. Hamesha apne browser me official pricing page khol kar dekhein ki aapko kaunsi currency dikh rahi hai — aur checkout par final amount (taxes ke saath) confirm karein. GetResponse ne 2 November ko Indian visitors ko INR dikhaya tha, lekin aaj uski page timeout hone se 10,000 contacts ki INR keemat verify nahi ho payi.

## Kaunsa tool kiske liye?

- **Sirf newsletter, budget zero:** **Kit Free ($0).** 10,000 subscribers tak broadcasts free hain. Jab welcome sequence ya automation chahiye, tab Creator ($139/mo) par jayein.
- **Mahine me 1–2 mail, sabse kam kharcha:** **Brevo Starter (₹2,505/mo).** Lekin yaad rakhein — frequency badhte hi bill badhega.
- **Automation + ecommerce, rozana mail:** **GetResponse Starter ($79/mo).** Unlimited sends ki wajah se high-volume par sabse sasta paid option hai, aur Marketer ($114/mo) par abandoned cart aur sales funnels mil jate hain.
- **Mailchimp Standard (₹8,360/mo):** tab lena jab aapko uske templates, integrations ya ecommerce ecosystem ki khaas zaroorat ho. Sirf keemat dekhein to ye sabse mehenga hai.
- **Creators jo courses/digital products bechte hain:** Kit Creator ($139/mo) ya GetResponse Creator ($134/mo) — dono me selling tools built-in hain; Kit ka free plan yahan bhi pehle validate karne ka rasta deta hai.

Aur ek practical tip: **list saaf rakhein.** Mailchimp aur GetResponse dono stored contacts par charge karte hain — inactive ya unsubscribed contacts ko archive karna seedha bill kam karta hai. Brevo par sends gin kar rakhein — har extra mail ka paisa lagta hai.
