## Seedhi baat pehle: 500 products ke liye host specs se chunein, intro price se nahi

Agar aap Google par "best hosting for woocommerce india 500 products" search karke yahan aaye hain, to seedha jawab ye hai: **500 products ke liye aapko ek aisa plan chahiye jisme kam se kam 4 GB RAM ke aas-paas resources, 100 ke aas-paas PHP workers, NVMe storage, object cache aur daily backups hon.** Product count apne aap me koi limit nahi hai — WooCommerce me products ki koi fixed limit nahi hoti. Asli limit hosting resources ki hoti hai: variations, filters, traffic aur plugins milkar database par load daalte hain, aur wahi load store ko slow karta hai.

Ye analysis public specs aur official pricing data ke basis par hai — 3 November 2026 ko Hostinger, MilesWeb aur Bluehost ke official WooCommerce pages live check kiye gaye hain. Humne in plans ko khud khareedkar test nahi kiya hai, isliye yahan koi "maine test kiya" wala daava nahi milega — sirf official page par jo likha hai, wahi, aur jahan official pages me kuch missing ya conflicting hai, wahan wahi khulkar likha hai.

Ek baat shuru me hi saaf kar dein: **cart, checkout aur account pages cache nahi hote.** Blog ke pages cache karke tez dikhaye ja sakte hain, lekin jab customer cart me product daalta hai ya payment karta hai, tab har request PHP chalata hai aur database ko hit karta hai. Isliye WooCommerce hosting, blog hosting se alag hoti hai — yahan PHP workers aur RAM hi asli performance decide karte hain, sirf storage nahi.

## 500 products wala store asal me kitna bhaari hota hai

500 products ka matlab sirf 500 rows nahi hota. Ek product ke saath uski images, variations (size, colour), attributes, categories, tags, reviews aur stock status judta hai. Agar aapke 500 products me se 100 variable products hain aur har ek ke 10 variations hain, to database me 1,000+ product entries jaisi load ban jaati hai. Us par customer jab filter lagata hai — "₹500 se kam, size M, in stock" — to wo query poori catalog ko chhant-ti hai.

Isliye guides ka sujhav hai:

- **PHP 8.1 ya usse upar** — WooCommerce ki official minimum requirement ke mutabiq, aur PHP 8.x purane versions se kaafi tez hai.
- **Kam se kam 256 MB PHP memory limit** — official minimum; plugin-heavy store ke liye 512 MB zyada safe maana jaata hai.
- **Object caching (Redis ya Memcached)** — recommended, kyunki ye baar-baar hone wale database queries ko kam karta hai.
- **Medium store (100–1,000 products) ke liye lagbhag 4 GB RAM** — third-party sizing guides ka aam sujhav.

Ye figures official docs aur guides ke summary par based hain, humne aaj woocommerce.com ka server-requirements page dobara kholkar verify nahi kiya hai — lekin ye numbers har bade guide me ek jaise milte hain, isliye inhe planning ke liye istemal kar sakte hain. Final authority aapke WordPress dashboard me WooCommerce → Status page hai, jo aapke server ko khud grade karta hai.

## India-specific cheezein: UPI, GST invoice aur support

**Payment gateway hosting se alag hai.** Koi bhi host aapko UPI gateway free me nahi deta — WooCommerce par Razorpay, Cashfree ya PayU ka official plugin lagana hota hai. Third-party reports ke mutabiq Razorpay ka standard domestic charge lagbhag 2% + GST hai (UPI par 0%), aur Cashfree ka standard domestic rate lagbhag 1.95% bataya jaata hai, eligible naye merchants ke liye ₹20 lakh tak 0% MDR ka offer 31 March 2027 tak bataya gaya hai. Ye figures gateway ki official pricing page par confirm karein — ye hosting price ka hissa nahi hain, aur har sale par kat-te hain.

**GST invoice** business ke liye zaroori hai: MilesWeb apne cards par "prices do not include GST (18%)" saaf likhta hai, Hostinger aur Bluehost ke cards par aisa note nahi mila — checkout par tax line aur GST invoice ka option dono check karein. **Hindi support** chahiye to MilesWeb ka explicit Hindi daava plus point hai; Hostinger aur Bluehost par aisa daava cards par nahi mila.

**Agar aapka store abhi 500 products se shuru ho raha hai aur budget tight hai**, to ye sequence samajhdari hai: MilesWeb Cloud Startup ya Hostinger Unlimited se shuru karein, pehle 3 mahine me WooCommerce → Status page par PHP memory aur object cache check karein, aur jaise hi daily orders 50 ke paar hon ya sale par checkout slow lage, turant Cloud Startup/Professional tier par upgrade karein. Upgrade ka raasta pehle se khula rakhna, sahi plan chunne se kam zaroori nahi hai.
