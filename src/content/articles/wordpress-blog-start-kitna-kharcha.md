## Sapna vs Haqeeqat: Blog Start Karna Kitna Sasta Hai?

Har beginner blogger ka sapna ek jaisa hota hai — "blog se lakhon kamayenge". Aur Google par pehla search hota hai: *"wordpress blog start kitna kharcha"*. Uske baad jo ads aur hosting pages dikhte hain, sab kehte hain "sirf ₹69/mo se shuru karo!"

Lekin yeh poori sachchai nahi hai. ₹69/mo wala number **intro price** hai — lambi term (36 ya 48 mahine) ke liye. Asli kharcha samajhne ke liye aapko **year-1 total** aur **renewal price** dono dekhne honge. Yeh article public specs, user reviews aur pricing data ke analysis ke basis par likha gaya hai — taaki aap pehle saal ke baad wale "renewal shock" me na phasein.

## Kharcha #1: Domain Name (~₹99–999 Pehla Saal)

Blog ka pehla kharcha hai domain name — aapke blog ka address. `.com` domain ka first-year promo aksar ₹99 se ₹999 tak mil jata hai, lekin renewal par typically ₹1,000–1,500/saal lagta hai. Yeh ek general market range hai, kisi ek company ka fixed quote nahi — actual price registrar aur promo par depend karta hai.

**Paise bachane ka tareeka:** Hostinger ke Premium (₹149/mo) aur "Unlimited" (₹249/mo) plans me **1 saal free domain** included hai. MilesWeb ke sabhi plans (Starter ₹69/mo se lekar Cloud Startup ₹399/mo tak) me bhi **1 saal free domain + free SSL** milta hai. Matlab pehle saal domain ka kharcha zero ho sakta hai — bas renewal ke liye tayyar rahiye.

Dhyan dijiye: Single plan ka ₹69/mo wala number **48 mahine ek saath pay karne par** milta hai — matlab checkout par aapko ~₹3,312 (48 × 69) ek saath dena hoga. Aur jab plan renew hoga, wahi ₹289/mo lagega — yani **4x price hike**.

Intro deal buri nahi hai — lekin sach yeh hai ki aap **₹3,312 upfront** de rahe hain. Budget ke hisaab se yeh decision lena important hai.

## Kharcha #3: Theme — Free vs Premium

WordPress ki sabse badi khasiyat: themes ka khazana. Beginners aksar premium theme par paise uda dete hain, lekin sach yeh hai:

- **Free themes (₹0):** Astra, GeneratePress, Kadence — fast, lightweight, aur blog ke liye kaafi. Pehle 6-12 mahine free theme par hi kaam chal jata hai.
- **Premium themes (₹3,000–6,000 one-time):** Yeh typical one-time payment range hai. Tabhi invest karein jab blog se traffic aur income aana start ho jaye.

**Verdict:** Shuruaat me theme par **₹0** kharch karein. Paise content me lagayein, design me nahi.

## Kharcha #4: Plugins — Mostly Free

WordPress plugins ke liye beginners ko alag se budget rakhne ki zaroorat nahi hai:

- **SEO:** Rank Math / Yoast — free version kaafi
- **Cache:** LiteSpeed Cache / WP Super Cache — free
- **Security:** Wordfence — free
- **Forms:** WPForms lite — free

Premium plugins (₹2,000–5,000/saal) tabhi chahiye jab blog grow kare. Starting me **₹0**.

## Kharcha #5: Content — Sabse Underrated Kharcha

Yeh woh kharcha hai jiske baare me koi nahi batata. Blog tabhi chalega jab content hoga:

- **Khud likhte hain:** ₹0 — lekin time lagta hai (har quality article 4-8 ghante)
- **Freelancer se likhwate hain:** ₹500–2,000 per article (market range, quality par depend)

Agar aap hafte me 2 article chahte hain aur khud nahi likh sakte, to content hi sabse bada monthly kharcha ban jata hai. Hamari salah: pehle 20-30 articles khud likhiye — topic expertise bhi banegi aur paise bhi bachenge.

## Apna Exact Kharcha Nikalo

Har blog alag hota hai — kisi ko 1 website chahiye, kisi ko 3, kisi ko domain alag se lena hai. Guesswork karne ke bajaye [apna exact kharcha nikalo](/tools/website-cost-calculator/) — hamara free calculator aapke choices ke hisaab se year-1 aur renewal dono ka total nikal kar dega. 2 minute ka kaam hai, aur renewal shock se bachne ka sabse aasan tareeka.

## Paise Bachane ke 3 Tareeke

1. **Lambi term ka dar mat khao, samjho:** 48 mahine ka intro deal tabhi lo jab aap blogging ko seriously 2+ saal dene wale ho. Warna 12 mahine ka plan lo aur renewal reality ko budget me rakho.
2. **Free domain offers ka fayda uthao:** Pehle saal ka ₹1,000–1,500 bachana chhoti baat nahi. Hostinger Premium/Unlimited aur MilesWeb ke sabhi plans me free domain milta hai.
3. **Theme aur plugin par ₹0:** Pehle saal design par ek rupaya mat kharch karo. Free theme + free plugins se blog fully functional hota hai. Paise content ya thode se marketing me lagao.

## Kab Upgrade Karein?

Shared hosting se shuruaat karna bilkul sahi hai. Upgrade tab karein jab:

- Roz ke **2,000–3,000 visitors** par site noticeably slow hone lage
- Aapko **3 se zyada websites** chalani hon (tab Hostinger Premium/Unlimited ya MilesWeb Business samajh aata hai)
- Blog se itni income aane lage ki ₹400–500/mo ka cloud hosting justified ho

Upgrade ka poora framework samajhne ke liye padhein: [Shared vs VPS vs Cloud Hosting — Beginners Guide](/shared-vs-vps-vs-cloud-hosting-beginners-guide/).

## Aakhri Baat

WordPress blog start karne ka **minimum honest kharcha ₹1,000–2,000 (pehla saal)** hai — agar aap khud content likhte hain aur free theme/plugins use karte hain. Comfortable setup (achi hosting + free domain) ke liye **₹2,000–4,000/saal** budget rakhein.

Sabse important lesson: **checkout se pehle hamesha renewal price check karein.** Intro price marketing hai, renewal price reality hai. Aur agar budget tight hai to same-renewal pricing wali company (jaise MilesWeb) chunna zyada safe hai — kam se kam koi surprise to nahi milega.
