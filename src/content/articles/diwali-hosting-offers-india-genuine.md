## Diwali aa rahi hai — hosting deal ka asli scene kya hai?

Diwali 2026 ab zyada door nahi: **Dhanteras 6 November, Lakshmi Puja 8 November**. Flipkart aur Amazon ki festive sales to pehle se live hain (unka detail comparison humne [yahan](/big-billion-days-vs-great-indian-festival-2026/) kiya hai), lekin hosting khareedne walon ka sabse bada sawal abhi bhi unanswered hai: **Diwali par hosting par asli discount milega ya sirf "up to 80% off" wale banners?**

## Renewal price aur GST: Diwali deal ka asli hisaab

Diwali banners par sabse bada dhokha renewal price me chhupa hota hai. Ek example:

- **Hostinger Single:** ₹69/mo × 48 months = ₹3,312 (pehla term). Renewal ₹289/mo par hota hai — matlab agle term me wahi plan ~₹13,872 ka padega. **4x ka jump.**
- **MilesWeb Advance:** ₹99/mo × 36 months = ₹3,564. Renewal bhi ₹99/mo — **zero jump.**

Aur dono me **18% GST upar se**: ₹99/mo ka plan checkout par ~₹117/mo, ₹149/mo ka plan ~₹176/mo ban jata hai. Domain bhi sirf pehle saal free hai — dusre saal se .com renewal ~₹800–1,200/saal alag se lagega.

**Genuine deal ka formula:** (intro price × term + GST) + (renewal price × expected saal + GST) + domain renewal = asli cost. Jo deal is hisaab me bhi sasti nikle, wahi genuine hai. Yehi calculation [website cost calculator](/tools/website-cost-calculator/) me karke dekho — banner dekhkar impress hone se behtar hai.

## Genuine deal pehchanne ke 5 niyam (Diwali-season edition)

1. **"Up to" ko hamesha question karo.** Headline discount sabse lambe term par hota hai. Apne billing term par actual per-month price checkout page par verify karo.
2. **Renewal price pehle dekho, discount baad me.** Pehle saal ka 80% off bekaar hai agar renewal 4x hai aur aap 3 saal rukne wale ho.
3. **Countdown timer se panic mat karo.** Flash-sale timers aksar recycle hote hain. Deal ka substance price me hai, timer me nahi.
4. **GST aur domain renewal jodna mat bhulo.** 18% GST aur dusre saal ka domain charge total cost ka 15–20% badha sakta hai.
5. **"Expected" aur "announced" me farq rakho.** Jab tak host ki official site ya official announcement me deal nahi dikhta, usko plan ka base mat banao — chahe kitni bhi sites uski date bata rahi hon.

Hosting ke alawa bhi Diwali-season me kharcha plan kar rahe ho to [small business ke liye GST invoice wali hosting guide](/shared-hosting-small-business-gst-invoice-india/) padho — usme billing aur invoice ka practical side cover hai. Aur agar Hostinger ke plans me confuse ho to [Single vs Premium ka detail comparison](/hostinger-single-vs-premium-plan/) dekho.

## Strategy: abhi lo ya Diwali (8 November) tak wait karo?

Teen tarah ke buyers, teen strategies:

- **Website abhi launch karni hai:** Wait mat karo. MilesWeb ki flash sale (Advance ₹99/mo + same-price renewal) aaj live hai aur Hostinger ke intro prices bhi. Diwali offers inhi base prices par extra perks add karte hain — base price ka wait karna time waste hai.
- **Launch me 2–3 hafte ki flexibility hai:** Diwali week (2–8 November) tak nazar rakho. Hosts aksar Diwali par Diwali-branded offers announce karte hain, aur 19 October ke aas-paas Hostinger ki festive window ki bhi khabar hai (unverified). Ek bookmark laga lo, hafte me ek baar official pages check karo.
- **Budget sabse tight hai, long-term soch rahe ho:** MilesWeb ka Advance (₹99/mo, same renewal) ya Starter (₹69/mo, same renewal) sabse predictable hai. Pehle saal ka sasta sticker dekhkar Hostinger le lena aur renewal par 4x dena — ye Diwali-season ka sabse common trap hai.

Ek middle path bhi hai: abhi 12-month term par lo, Diwali/Black Friday sale me lamba term extend kar lo. Zyadaatar hosts existing customers ko bhi sale prices par upgrade dete hain.
