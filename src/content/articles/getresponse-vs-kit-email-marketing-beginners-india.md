Email marketing shuru karne wale har beginner ke saamne ek hi sawaal hota hai: free me shuru karein ya paid plan lein — aur kis tool se? India me do naam sabse zyada sunne ko milte hain: **GetResponse** (all-in-one marketing platform) aur **Kit** (pehle ConvertKit, creators ke liye bana email tool).

Dono ke free plans hain, dono ke paid plans subscriber count par based hain, aur dono ke daawe bade hain. Is guide me hum official pricing pages aur help docs se verify kiye gaye facts rakhenge — koi "maine test kiya" wala jhootha daawa nahi.

**Honesty note:** Ye article public pricing pages, official FAQ/help pages aur Oct 2026 ke independent reviews ke analysis par based hai. Jahan official page par confirm nahi hua, wahan saaf likha hai.

## Research methodology

Saari prices 29 Oct 2026 ko official pricing pages se verify ki gayi hain: GetResponse ki pricing page (list size 1,000, monthly aur yearly -18% rates) aur Kit ki pricing page (1,000 subscribers, yearly toggle). Dono tools ke screenshots bhi aaj hi capture kiye gaye hain. Payment options GetResponse ki official payment help page se liye gaye hain. Free plan ke kuch limits (jaise GetResponse ke 500 contacts) Oct 2026 ke multiple independent reviews me consistent hain — official page unhe seedha nahi dikhata, isliye article me unhe reported limits ke roop me likha gaya hai. Prices USD me hain aur GetResponse ki prices net amounts hain — local taxes checkout par add ho sakte hain.

## Free plan: Kit kaafi aage, lekin ek catch ke saath

**Kit free plan** email marketing ke sabse generous free tiers me se ek hai:

- 10,000 subscribers tak free (sirf unique aur active subscribers count hote hain)
- Unlimited landing pages, unlimited forms, unlimited broadcasts
- Audience tagging aur segmentation
- Digital products aur subscriptions bech sakte ho (3.5% + 30c fee per sale)
- Newsletter feed aur website

**GetResponse free account** iske saamne chhota lagta hai:

- 500 contacts aur 2,500 newsletters/month tak (Oct 2026 ke independent reviews ke mutabiq)
- Pehle 14 din premium features try kar sakte ho, uske baad limits lag jaati hain
- Har message par GetResponse badge lagta hai (paid plan par hat-ta hai)
- Landing page visits 1,000 unique visitors/month tak seemit
- Trial ke dauran webinars sirf 10 logon tak

Lekin yahan ek ulta twist hai: **GetResponse ke free account me workflows banaye ja sakte hain** (dynamic segment filter ke bina), jabki **Kit ke free plan me official pricing table ke mutabiq visual automation builder included nahi hai** — automation Creator plan se shuru hota hai. Kuch purane guides me Kit free me 1 automation ka zikr milta hai, lekin current official page ke hisaab se aisa nahi hai. Isliye "free me automation" chahiye to GetResponse ka free account thoda aage hai, "free me bada list size" chahiye to Kit.

## Automation: dono ka gate alag hai

Automation hi woh feature hai jo beginner ko sabse zyada confuse karta hai, kyunki dono tools is par alag tareeke se gate lagate hain:

- **GetResponse Starter ($19/mo): sirf 1 custom automation workflow.** Welcome series chala li to doosri sequence ke liye upgrade karna padega. Unlimited workflows ke liye Marketer ($59/mo) chahiye.
- **Kit Creator ($39/mo): unlimited visual automations aur unlimited sequences.** Free plan me automation builder nahi hai, lekin ek baar Creator par aa gaye to automation par koi count limit nahi.
- **Kit Creator Pro ($79/mo):** advanced A/B testing (5 subject lines tak), engagement scoring, deliverability reporting aur referral system add hota hai.

Seedha matlab: agar aapko 2-3 automated sequences chahiye (welcome + launch + re-engagement), to GetResponse me aapko $59/mo dena padega, jabki Kit me $39/mo me kaam ho jayega. Lekin Kit me automation ke liye free plan kaafi nahi hai.

## Landing pages, forms aur bechna: kaun kya deta hai

**Landing pages aur forms:** Dono me unlimited landing pages aur forms milte hain (Kit free me bhi unlimited; GetResponse me paid plans par unlimited, free account par visits ki limit hai). Kit ka edge ye hai ki uska newsletter feed aur website free plan me bhi milta hai — apna blog/newsletter ghar banane ke liye achha.

**Digital products bechna:** Kit me aap free plan se hi digital products, paid newsletters aur subscriptions bech sakte ho — fee 3.5% + 30c per sale hai, jisme Kit ka apna hissa sirf 0.6% hai. GetResponse me selling tools (sales funnels, promo codes) Marketer se shuru hote hain, aur courses/webinars Creator plan me hain.

**Webinars aur courses:** Yahan GetResponse clear winner hai. Creator plan ($69/mo) me webinars (100 attendees tak), course creator (500 students tak) aur paid newsletters ek hi dashboard me mil jaate hain — alag webinar tool ka kharcha bach jaata hai. Kit me native webinar hosting nahi hai; aapko webinars ke liye doosra tool jodna padega.

## India se payment: card zaroori, UPI confirmed nahi

Ye section Indian beginners ke liye sabse practical hai:

- **GetResponse:** Official payment help page ke mutabiq Visa, MasterCard, Discover, Maestro, JCB aur American Express cards accepted hain, plus Apple Pay aur Google Pay. **India me PayPal aur PayU bhi accepted hain.** Card par recurring aur international transactions enabled hona zaroori hai — GetResponse khud ye warn karta hai.
- **Kit:** Plans card se Stripe ke through pay hote hain. International-transaction-enabled card chahiye.
- **UPI:** Na GetResponse ki official payment list me UPI hai, na Kit ke liye UPI confirmed hai. Kisi third-party site par UPI ka daawa dikhe to use official confirmation mat samjho. Safe tareeka: ek international card rakho, ya GetResponse ke liye PayPal/PayU try karo.

Ek aur India-specific baat: dono tools USD me bill karte hain. Aapke card ka exchange rate aur bank ka forex markup final cost badha sakta hai — isliye annual plan lene se pehle ek mahine ka actual charge check kar lo.

## Growth ke saath cost kaise badhti hai

List badhne par dono ki pricing alag behave karti hai:

| List size | GetResponse Starter | GetResponse Marketer | Kit Creator (monthly) | Kit Pro (monthly) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1,000 | $19/mo | $59/mo | $39/mo | $79/mo |
| 5,000 | $54/mo | $95/mo | $89/mo | $139/mo |
| 10,000 | $79/mo | $114/mo | $139/mo | $189/mo |

Do baatein dhyaan rakhne layak hain:

1. **Kit ka $0 → $139 jump:** Free plan 10,000 subscribers par khatam hota hai aur aap seedha $139/mo wale band par jaate ho. Koi dheere-dheere badhne wala rasta nahi hai.
2. **GetResponse me gap kam hota hai:** 1,000 contacts par Marketer Starter se 3x mehnga hai, lekin 10,000 par sirf ~44% zyada. Badi list par Marketer justify karna aasan ho jaata hai.

Annual billing par Kit "2 months free" deta hai (Creator $390/yr), aur GetResponse 18% discount deta hai. Lekin annual plan tabhi lo jab tool se sure ho — dono ki refund policies sakht ho sakti hain, isliye pehle monthly par test karo.

## Kaunsa kiske liye: seedha decision guide

**Kit lo, agar:**
- Aap abhi free me shuru karna chahte ho aur 10,000 subscribers tak bina paise diye list banana chahte ho
- Aap creator ho — writer, YouTuber, coach, newsletter publisher
- Aap digital products ya paid newsletter bechna chahte ho bina kisi paid plan ke
- Aapko tagging-based segmentation pasand hai

**GetResponse lo, agar:**
- Aapko shuru se automation + webinars + courses ek hi tool me chahiye
- Aap ecommerce ya sales funnels chalate ho (abandoned cart, promo codes)
- Aapko India me PayPal/PayU se pay karna hai
- Aapki list pehle se badi hai aur aapko unlimited sends chahiye (paid plans par sends unlimited hain)

**Dono mat lo, agar:** Aapko sirf kabhi-kabhi email bhejna hai aur automation ki zaroorat hi nahi — tab ek simple free newsletter tool bhi kaafi hoga, aur paise bachenge.
