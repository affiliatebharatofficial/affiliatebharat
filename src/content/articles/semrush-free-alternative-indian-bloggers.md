## Semrush Ka Free Account: Kitna Kaam Ka?

Paid plan lene se pehle Semrush ka **free account** bhi hai — bina credit card ke, bina expiry ke. Official free-account page aur pricing page ke mutabiq isme milta hai:

- **Roz 10 reports** — Domain Overview, Keyword Research, Competitor Research, Backlinks — har tool par 10 queries/day
- **1 project** (1 website) aur **10 tracked keywords**
- **Site Audit: 100 pages** tak
- SEO Ideas par limited access

Ye ek blogger ke liye kaafi hai jo hafte me 2-3 baar ek keyword ya ek competitor check karta hai. Lekin rozana workflow ke liye nahi: subah 10 lookups dopahar tak khatam ho jayenge, aur 10 keywords ki tracking se ek badhta hua blog cover nahi hota.

**7-day free trial** alag cheez hai: isme poora paid plan 7 din ke liye milta hai, lekin credit card dena zaroori hai aur 7 din ke baad auto-bill shuru ho jata hai. Trial tabhi lo jab ek hafte ka SEO kaam pehle se ready ho. Ek reported risk bhi hai: kuch third-party guides ke mutabiq trial ya paid plan lene ke baad free account ki eligibility khatam ho sakti hai — ye official page par confirm nahi hua, isliye isko unverified warning ke roop me lo.

## Free Alternative #1: Google Search Console (Sabse Zaroori)

Agar sirf ek free tool chunna ho to woh **Google Search Console (GSC)** hai. Ye Google ka apna official tool hai, poori tarah free, aur iski koi usage limit nahi hai. Isme aapko milta hai:

- Aapki site kin keywords par Google me dikh rahi hai, kin par clicks aa rahe hain
- Kaunse pages index hue, kaunse me error hai
- Mobile usability aur Core Web Vitals issues
- Kaunsi sites aapko link kar rahi hain

**GSC ki seema:** ye sirf aapki apni verified sites ka data dikhata hai. Competitor kis keyword par rank kar raha hai, kisi keyword ki difficulty kitni hai, ya naye keyword ideas — ye sab GSC nahi batata. Isliye GSC ko base banao, uske upar ek keyword tool jodo.

Ek practical note: GSC ka dashboard Google login ke peeche hota hai (aapki apni site ka private data), isliye is article me iska screenshot nahi diya gaya hai — iska public page {{aff:google-search-console|Google Search Console}} par dekha ja sakta hai.

## Free Alternative #2: Ubersuggest Free Tier

Neil Patel ka **Ubersuggest** Indian bloggers ke beech sabse popular budget tool hai, kyunki iska interface simple hai aur free tier me bhi kaafi data milta hai. Third-party pricing coverage (Zapier, Backlinko, October 2026 tak verified) ke mutabiq free plan me milta hai:

- **Roz 3 searches** — keyword ideas, site audit, domain overview, sab mil kar 3
- **1 project** aur lagbhag **25 tracked keywords**
- **Free Chrome extension** jo Google, YouTube aur Amazon ke results par keyword data dikhata hai (roz lagbhag 40 overlay searches tak)

Ubersuggest ki taakat ye hai ki har search me bahut data milta hai: monthly search volume, SEO difficulty score, paid difficulty, content ideas aur SERP analysis. Kamzori ye hai ki 3 searches/day bahut jaldi khatam ho jate hain, aur long-tail keywords par iska data Semrush jitna deep nahi mana jata.

Paid plans third-party coverage ke mutabiq $29/mo se shuru hote hain (Individual), aur ek lifetime deal bhi milta hai — official pricing page par current prices confirm kar lo, kyunki promotions ke saath ye badalte rehte hain.

## Jo Free Tools Replace NAHI Kar Sakte (Honest Section)

Free stack bahut kuch cover karta hai, lekin kuch Semrush features ka koi free equivalent nahi hai — ye jaanna zaroori hai taaki galat ummeed na pale:

- **Keyword Gap at scale:** ek saath sau keywords par apni site vs competitor ka gap dekhna free tools me nahi hota.
- **Historical data:** pichhle 2 saal me competitor kis keyword par kab rank karta tha — ye Guru/Pro+ level feature hai.
- **Content optimization toolkit:** article likhte waqt real-time SEO suggestions (Pro+ se upar) ka free version nahi hai.
- **Backlink database ki depth:** Semrush ka backlink index industry me sabse deep mana jata hai; free tools me sirf apni site ke links poori tarah dikhte hain.
- **API aur white-label reporting:** client work ke liye chahiye hota hai, free me nahi milta.

Ek bonus free option: **Ahrefs Webmaster Tools** — apni verified sites ke liye free backlink data aur 5,000 pages tak ka site audit deta hai (third-party coverage ke mutabiq). Competitor data isme bhi nahi milta, lekin apni site ke backlinks ke liye ye GSC ka accha saathi hai.
