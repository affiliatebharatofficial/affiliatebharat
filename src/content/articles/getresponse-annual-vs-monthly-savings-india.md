Ye wo baat hai jo pehli baar GetResponse khareedne wale ko confuse karti hai. GetResponse ki list price US dollars me hai. Indian visitors ko page rupayon me price dikhata hai, aur page par saaf likha hai ke displayed prices current exchange rate par depend karti hain — aur "prices above are net amounts" hai, matlab local taxes alag se lag sakte hain.

Iska asar aap khud dekh sakte ho:

- **18 November 2026:** yearly Starter ₹1,466.24/mo (monthly ₹1,788.10)
- **24 November 2026:** yearly Starter ₹1,448.73/mo (monthly ₹1,766.75)

Dollar price dono din wahi tha — $15.58 yearly, $19 monthly. Sirf exchange rate hila: 18 November ko lagbhag ₹95 per dollar, aaj lagbhag ₹93 per dollar. Matlab aap jis din khareedte ho, us din ka rate aapki bill tay karta hai. Annual plan par ye farak aur bhi bada hota hai, kyunki poora saal ka paisa ek hi din ke rate par lock ho jata hai.

**Practical tip:** agar rupee dollar ke mukable strong ho raha hai to annual khareedne ka ye achha waqt ho sakta hai — aapki INR bill us din ke rate par freeze ho jayegi. Aur checkout se pehle ye bhi check karo ke aapke card par international transaction aur forex markup kya lagta hai — ye 2–3.5% tak ho sakta hai, jo aapki 18% bachat me se ek hissa kha jata hai.

## Annual Lene Se Pehle: Teen Traps

### 1. List badh jaye to bachat ghis jati hai

Annual billing ki 18% chhoot is assumption par likhi hai ke aapki list poora saal ek hi bracket me rahegi. GetResponse ki terms ke mutabiq, agar aapki list saal ke beech me apne bracket se upar chali jaye to extra contacts ka charge **monthly basis par alag se** lagta hai — ya phir aap upgrade karte ho to usi din se **naya 12-mahine ka term** shuru ho jata hai. Matlab aapne jo annual plan January me liya tha, June me upgrade karte hi uska term phir se shuru — purana bacha hua period adjust hota hai, lekin aap ek aur saal ke liye lock ho jate ho.

Isliye annual tabhi lo jab aapko lagbhag pakka ho ke list agle 12 mahine me bracket nahi badlegi. Agar aap ads chala rahe ho, lead magnet viral ho sakta hai, ya list har mahine 10–15% badh rahi hai — to monthly se shuru karo, aur jab growth stable ho jaye tab annual par switch karo.

### 2. Downgrade aasan nahi hai

Official FAQ ke mutabiq upgrade kabhi bhi kar sakte ho — billing cycle usi din se nayi shuru hoti hai. Lekin **downgrade ya annual se monthly par wapas jaana sirf Support Team ko ticket bhejkar hota hai** — khud se dashboard se nahi. Aur list chhoti ho jane par koi automatic refund nahi milta. Independent review ke mutabiq GetResponse ka help center kehta hai ke service pay-as-you-go hai aur cancellation ke baad bhi refund nahi deta — chahe aapne charge hone ke turant baad cancel kiya ho. Ye humne khud help center se verify nahi kiya hai, isliye isko reported ke roop me lein — lekin annual lene se pehle is rule ko dhyaan me rakhna chahiye.

### 3. "30% off on 2-year billing" — standard plans par nahi

Kuch third-party guides kehte hain ke 2 saal ke liye pay karne par lagbhag 30% ki chhoot milti hai. Official FAQ isse contradict karta hai: **24-month billing sirf Enterprise plan par milti hai** — Starter, Marketer aur Creator par sirf monthly ya 12-month. Agar koi guide aapko standard plan par 2-year discount ka vaada kare to wo galat hai. Enterprise quote-only hai, isliye uski keemat yahan compare nahi ki ja sakti.

### Free custom domain

### 14-day free trial — bina card ke

Pricing page ka headline hai: "Try free for 14 days. No credit card required." Trial me premium features milte hain, aur 14 din ke baad account free version par aa jata hai. Free plan 500 contacts aur 2,500 emails per month tak ka hai (ye figure independent readings se hai — aaj pricing page par koi alag free-plan card nahi dikh raha, sirf trial ka headline hai).

Trial ka sahi use ye hai: **pehle trial lo, phir annual lo.** Trial ke 14 din me apni list import karo, ek automation workflow banao, ek campaign bhejo — aur tab faisla karo ke kaunsa plan chahiye. Trial ke baad seedha annual lena matlab bina aazmaye saal bhar ka paisa bandhna.

### NGO discount: 50% — annual se bhi behtar

Agar aap NGO ya nonprofit chalate ho to GetResponse ki official nonprofit page par **har monthly plan par 50% ki chhoot** hai, 24 mahine ke liye. Shartein: NGO status confirm hona chahiye, political organisations eligible nahi hain, aur 4 hafte ke andar GetResponse ka logo apni website par lagana padta hai (warna discount agle billing cycle se hat jata hai). List 100,000 contacts tak ki ho sakti hai.

Lekin ek catch hai: **ye discount annual billing ke saath combine nahi hota.** NGO ko monthly plan lena padta hai — jo 50% chhoot ke saath annual ki 18% se kahin behtar hai. Starter monthly $19 ka 50% matlab $9.50/mo — annual ke $15.58 se bhi kam.

### Contract buyout

Agar aap kisi aur email tool ke contract me bandhe ho to GetResponse ki "Contract Buyout" offer hai — qualifying customers ke liye wo aapke purane contract ki bachi hui keemat cover karte hain aur migration me madad karte hain. Ye har kisi ko nahi milta — terms individually agree hote hain — lekin agar aap Mailchimp ya Brevo se switch kar rahe ho to sales team se poochna banta hai.
