## Hindi blog ke liye ye comparison kyun alag hai

Google par "hostinger vs bluehost" search karo to hazaron generic comparisons mil jayenge — sab English audience ke liye, sab me wahi copy-paste feature lists. Lekin ek Hindi blogger ki zarooratein thodi alag hoti hain:

- **Budget tight hota hai.** Pehla blog aksar side-hustle hota hai — har mahine ka ₹100–200 ka farq bhi matter karta hai.

## Renewal price ka sach — jo ads nahi batate

Hosting companies ka business model simple hai: pehle saal sasta do, dusre saal se asli price lo. Isme kuch galat nahi hai — lekin buyer ke taur par aapko ye **pehle se** pata hona chahiye.

**Hostinger** is mamle me transparent hai: Single plan ₹69/mo se renew hokar ₹289/mo (lagbhag 4x), aur Premium ₹149/mo se ₹449/mo (lagbhag 3x) ho jaata hai. Numbers official site par likhe hain, to surprise nahi hona chahiye — bas kharidte time inhe dhyan me rakho.

**Bluehost** me twist hai: intro prices to saaf-saaf diye hain (Basic ₹299/mo, Choice Plus ₹499/mo — dono 36-month term par), lekin **renewal prices official site par publish hi nahi hain**. Help page par sirf itna likha hai ki promotions initial term ke liye hain. Matlab dusre term ka bill kitna aayega, ye aapko kharidne ke baad hi pata chalega. Ye ek honest red flag hai — hamari salah hai ki buy karne se pehle Bluehost support se renewal price likhwa lo.

**Teesra option — MilesWeb:** iska model hi alag hai. Starter ₹69/mo, Business ₹199/mo, Cloud Startup ₹399/mo — aur **renewal par bhi wahi price, guaranteed**. Koi renewal shock nahi. Ek caveat: in prices me 18% GST alag se lagta hai, to final bill thoda upar jayega — lekin wo bhi predictable hai.

## Kaun kiske liye: final decision

Ab seedhi salah, category-wise:

**Beginner Hindi blogger (pehla blog, tight budget) → Hostinger Single ya Premium.** Intro price sabse kam hai, interface beginner-friendly mana jaata hai, aur 1-click WordPress setup me koi dikkat nahi aati. Bas renewal price (₹289/mo ya ₹449/mo) pehle se note kar lo taaki dusre saal shock na lage. 48-month term lene par intro price lambe time tak lock rehta hai.

**Jisko brand name aur "safe choice" chahiye → Bluehost.** Bluehost WordPress.org par officially recommended raha hai aur iska naam sunne me bharosa deta hai. Lekin hamari honest salah: **buy karne se pehle renewal price support se confirm kar lo**, kyunki official site par ye publish nahi hai.

**Jisko Hindi support chahiye ya renewal shock se nafrat hai → MilesWeb.** Hindi me 24/7 support aur same-price renewal — ye combination Hindi bloggers ke liye genuinely valuable hai. Business plan ₹199/mo (+ GST) me 50 sites tak ki capacity milti hai, jo future growth ke liye kaafi hai.
