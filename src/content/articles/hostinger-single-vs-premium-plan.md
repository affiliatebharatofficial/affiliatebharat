## 48 Month Ka Asli Hisaab: Intro Price vs Renewal

Hostinger ki pricing ka sabse important funda samajh lo: **jo price aapko pehle dikhta hai, woh intro price hai.** Renewal par price kaafi badh jata hai. Chalo 4 saal (48 month) ka poora math nikaalte hain:

### Single Plan — 4 saal ka kharcha

- **Intro (48 month):** ₹69 × 48 = **₹3,312**
- **Renewal ke baad:** ₹289/month (yaani har mahine intro se ₹220 zyada)

### Premium Plan — 4 saal ka kharcha

- **Intro (48 month):** ₹149 × 48 = **₹7,152**
- **Renewal ke baad:** ₹449/month (yaani har mahine intro se ₹300 zyada)

### Dono ka difference

- 4 saal me total difference: ₹7,152 − ₹3,312 = **₹3,840**
- Per month difference: ₹3,840 ÷ 48 = **sirf ₹80/month**

Ye ₹80/month woh rakam hai jo aapko sochni hai. ₹80 me aapko milta hai: 2 extra websites, double storage (10 GB → 20 GB), aur 1 saal ka free domain clearly included. Agar aap 2-3 projects ka soch bhi rahe ho, to ye ₹80/month ek solid investment hai.

Lekin ek warning bhi hai: **renewal par dono plans me price jump hota hai.** Single ₹69 se ₹289 (4x se zyada!) aur Premium ₹149 se ₹449 (3x). Isliye smart strategy ye hai ki 48-month ka term lekar intro price ko maximum time tak lock kar lo. 4 saal ek website ke liye lamba time hota hai, aur tab tak aapki site se earning shuru ho jani chahiye jo renewal cost cover kar de.

## Kaun Sa Plan Kiske Liye Best Hai?

Ab seedhi baat — aap kaun ho, us hisaab se plan chuno:

### Single plan lo, agar:

- Aapko **sirf 1 website** chahiye — ek blog, ek portfolio, ya ek chhoti business site
- Budget bilkul tight hai aur har rupaya matter karta hai
- Aap pehli baar website bana rahe ho aur experiment kar rahe ho
- 10 GB SSD aapke liye enough hai (ek typical WordPress blog aaram se 2-5 GB me chal jata hai)

**Example:** Rohan apna pehla Hindi tech blog shuru kar raha hai. Uske paas ek hi domain idea hai, budget ₹3,500 ke aas-paas hai. Uske liye Single perfect hai — ₹3,312 me 4 saal ka hosting sorted.

### Premium plan lo, agar:

- Aapke paas **2-3 website ideas** hain (blog + portfolio + client ka kaam, ya Hindi + English version)
- Future me expand karne ka plan hai — aaj 1 site, 6 mahine baad doosri
- 20 GB storage chahiye (zyada images, ya thodi heavy site)
- Free domain 1 saal ke liye clearly chahiye bina kisi confusion ke

**Example:** Priya ek food blog chalati hai aur ab ek recipe ebook wali site bhi launch karna chahti hai. Dono sites alag-alag hosting par lene se double kharcha hota — Premium me 3 sites ek hi plan me cover ho jati hain.

### Quick decision rule

> **"Ek site, pakka wala plan? → Single. Do site ka khayal bhi aaya? → Premium."**

₹80/month ka difference itna chhota hai ki agar aapke dimaag me doosri site ka idea *kahin bhi* hai, to Premium lena zyada samajhdari hai. Baad me alag se doosra Single plan lene se to Premium pehle se hi sasta padta hai.

## Renewal Trap: Ye Baat Zaroor Jaano

Ab woh baat jo hosting companies zor se nahi batatin: **renewal pricing.**

Hostinger akela nahi hai — lagbhag har shared hosting company intro price kam aur renewal price zyada rakhti hai. Lekin Hostinger ka jump kaafi noticeable hai:

- **Single:** ₹69 → ₹289/mo (**4.2x jump**)
- **Premium:** ₹149 → ₹449/mo (**3x jump**)

Iska matlab ye nahi ki Hostinger bura hai. Iska matlab ye hai ki **aapko pehle din se renewal ka plan bana kar chalna hai.** Kuch practical tips:

1. **48-month term lo.** Intro rate 4 saal tak lock ho jata hai. Chhote term (12 ya 24 month) par monthly rate zyada hota hai aur renewal jaldi aa jata hai.
2. **Auto-renewal ki date calendar me note karo.** Renewal se 1-2 mahine pehle Hostinger ke offers check karo — existing customers ko kabhi-kabhi discount milta hai.
3. **4 saal me site se earning ka target rakho.** Agar aapki site 4 saal me ₹449/month bhi nahi kama pa rahi, to problem hosting price me nahi, strategy me hai.
4. **Support se negotiate karne me sharmao mat.** Renewal ke time support chat par politely discount maangna ek common aur legitimate practice hai.

Ek aur honest baat: kuch log renewal price dekh kar host badalne ki sochte hain. Migration mehnat ka kaam hai aur downtime ka risk hota hai. Pehle din se sahi term chunna, baar-baar migrate karne se hamesha better hai.

## Baad Me Upgrade Kar Sakte Ho — Koi Tension Nahi

Sabse common sawal: *"Agar maine Single le liya aur baad me doosri site chahiye hui to?"*

Good news: **Hostinger plan upgrade support karta hai.** Aap Single se Premium (ya Premium se Unlimited) me baad me move kar sakte ho, aur price ka difference adjust ho jata hai. Iska matlab:

- **Pehle din se over-buy karne ki zaroorat nahi.** Budget tight hai? Single se start karo, befikar.
- **Growth par upgrade karo.** Jab doosri site ka time aaye, tab Premium me upgrade kar lena.
- **Lekin math yaad rakho:** Baad me upgrade karne par aapko prevailing rates par difference dena pad sakta hai. Agar aapko *pehle se pata hai* ki 6 mahine me doosri site aayegi, to pehle din se Premium lena hi sasta padta hai.

Upgrade ka process simple hai — Hostinger ke hPanel me plan change ka option milta hai, aur support bhi help kar deta hai. Koi website data loss nahi hota, sab kuch same server environment me rehta hai.
