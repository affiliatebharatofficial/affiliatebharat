## HubSpot Free CRM: sabse complete free plan, 2 logon tak

HubSpot ka free CRM is list ka sabse bada naam hai — aur iski sabse badi taakat ye hai ki free plan me sirf contacts store karna nahi, poora chhota sales cycle cover hota hai: contact management, deal pipeline, email tracking, meeting scheduling, live chat, forms aur reporting dashboard. Official page par aaj bhi saaf likha hai: "100% free with no expiration date" aur "No credit card required."

![HubSpot ki free CRM page 15 November 2026 — "Free CRM Software for Startups & Small Businesses", "100% free with no expiration date", "No credit card required"](/screenshots/hubspot-free-crm-2026.png)
*HubSpot ki free CRM page 15 November 2026 ko — free plan ki expiry nahi hai aur credit card nahi mangta*

Lekin free plan ki asli limits woh hain jo homepage par nahi, pricing page par milti hain — aur ye limits October–November 2026 me HubSpot ki pricing pages ki kai independent readings me ek jaisi mili hain:

- **2 users** aur **1,000 contacts** (September 2024 ke baad bane accounts par)
- **1 deal pipeline** — doosri pipeline chahiye to paid plan
- **2,000 marketing emails mahine me**, HubSpot ki branding ke saath
- **3 email templates** aur **1 meetings link**
- Sirf **ek automated action** — matlab welcome email jaisi basic automation bhi limited hai

Purane guides me HubSpot ke free plan ko "unlimited contacts" likha milta hai — woh September 2024 se pehle ki baat hai, jab limit 10 lakh contacts thi. Aaj ke hisab se woh galat hai, isliye us par bharosa karke 5,000 contacts import karne ki koshish na karein — 1,001st contact par rukawat aayegi.

**Paid step:** Sales Hub Starter **$15/seat/month annual billing** par ($20 monthly billing par) hai. Page par Professional "from $50" aur Enterprise "from $75" dikhate hain, lekin ye Customer Platform bundle ki starting keematein lagti hain — Sales Hub alag se lene par Professional $90/seat/month tak ja sakta hai (October 2026 readings). Isliye upgrade ka budget banate waqt checkout par dikh rahi keemat ko hi final maanein. Bharatiya users ke liye ek aur baat: HubSpot ki keematein dollar me hain, aur Indian checkout par INR dikhega ya USD, ye aaj verify nahi hua — card ka forex charge bhi hisaab me rakhein.

**Kiske liye:** 1–2 logon ki team, 1,000 se kam contacts, aur jise email marketing bhi free me chahiye. Freelancers, consultants aur chhote agencies ke liye ye sabse safe shuruat hai.

## Zoho CRM Free: 3 logon ki team ke liye best, Hindi bhi

Zoho CRM Bharat me sabse zyada use hone wala CRM hai — aur iski wajah sirf keemat nahi, India-first design hai: INR billing, GST invoice, Indian phone formats aur Hindi interface. Zoho ki pricing page ki FAQ aaj 15 November 2026 ko live padhi gayi, jisme saaf likha hai ki free edition **3 users tak** support karta hai, aur Zoho "kabhi paid plan par jaane ke liye force nahi karega."

*Zoho CRM ki pricing page 15 November 2026 ko — India visitors ko rupee keematein dikhti hain, annual billing par*

Free edition me kya milta hai (October–November 2026 ki kai readings ek jaisi hain):

- **3 users**, hamesha ke liye free, bina credit card ke
- **5,000 records** (10 MB) — contacts, deals, tasks sab milakar
- **5 active workflow rules** — follow-up reminders aur lead assignment jaise automation free me; HubSpot free me ye nahi milta
- **Rozana 150 emails**, **10 email templates**, **1 GB file storage**
- **5,000 API calls rozana** — apne tools se jodne ke liye
- Sirf **default pipeline** — extra pipelines paid plans me

Zoho ki FAQ ye bhi confirm karti hai ki CRM **28 languages** support karta hai, jinme **Hindi** shamil hai. Matlab team ka woh member jo English me comfortable nahi hai, woh bhi Hindi interface me kaam kar sakta hai — chhote Bharatiya businesses ke liye ye chhoti baat nahi hai.

**Paid step:** India keematein aaj live render me confirm hui — Standard **₹800/user/month**, Professional **₹1,400**, Enterprise **₹2,400** aur Ultimate **₹2,600** (annual billing, GST alag). GST-registered business input tax credit se GST wapas claim kar sakta hai, to asar me keemat ₹2,400/month ke aas-paas rehti hai.

**Kiske liye:** 3 logon ki team jo automation bhi free me chahti hai, aur jise INR billing + GST invoice chahiye. Coaching institutes, real estate agents aur chhote B2B teams ke liye ye default choice hai.

## Bharat wale sawal: INR, GST, UPI, Hindi aur WhatsApp

**INR billing:** Zoho CRM, Bigin aur Bitrix24 ki India pages par rupee keematein dikhti hain — aaj live render me confirm hua. HubSpot aur Freshsales ki pages dollar me hain; Indian checkout par currency kya dikhegi, ye verify nahi hua.

**GST:** Zoho aur Bigin ki keematein GST se pehle ki hain; 18% GST invoice par judta hai. GST-registered business input tax credit claim kar sakta hai. Bitrix24 ki page par bhi likha hai ki local taxes (VAT/GST) locality ke hisab se lagenge.

**UPI payment:** Zoho ki FAQ me payment methods me Visa, MasterCard, American Express, PayPal aur bank transfer likhe hain — **UPI ka zikr nahi hai**. HubSpot aur Bitrix24 ki pages par bhi UPI listed nahi hai. Isliye paid plan lete waqt international card ya PayPal ka intezam pehle se rakhein.

**Hindi interface:** Zoho CRM 28 languages support karta hai, jinme Hindi shamil hai — ye official FAQ se confirm hai. Baaki tools ki Hindi support ke baare me official pages par koi saaf daava nahi mila, isliye use claim nahi kiya ja raha.

**WhatsApp:** Bigin me WhatsApp Business integration Express plan (₹400/user/month) se shuru hoti hai. Zoho CRM me WhatsApp integration paid plans aur alag add-ons se milti hai — free plan me iski ummeed na rakhein. Agar aapki sales WhatsApp par hoti hai, to ye ek deciding factor ho sakta hai.

## Free plan par zyada din kaise tikein

1. **Contacts gin kar import karein.** HubSpot par 1,000 aur Zoho par 5,000 records ki limit sabse pehle khatm hoti hai. Purani, dead leads ko pehle saaf karein — har dead record free limit khata hai.
2. **Ek pipeline se shuru karein.** Saare free plans me ek hi pipeline milti hai. Alag-alag products ke liye alag pipeline banane ki koshish me paid plan ki zaroorat jaldi padegi.
3. **Automation ka budget tay karein.** Zoho free me 5 workflow rules deta hai — unhe sabse repeat hone wale kaam par lagayein (jaise naye lead par follow-up reminder). HubSpot free me automation lagbhag nahi hai, to wahan manual follow-up ka system pehle se bana lein.
4. **Export ka backup rakhein.** Free plan ho ya paid, apne contacts ka CSV export mahine me ek baar nikaal lein. Freshsales jaisi situation — trial khatm, account suspend — me data nikaalna mushkil ho sakta hai.
5. **Upgrade ka trigger pehle se likh lein.** Jaise "jab contacts 800 paar karenge, HubSpot Starter ka budget banayenge" — isse beech me panic me galat plan nahi lena padega.
