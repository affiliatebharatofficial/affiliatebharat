## ₹69/mo wala plan dusre saal ₹289/mo kyun ho jata hai

Hosting khareedte time sabse bada dhokha renewal price ka hota hai. Aapne Hostinger ka Single plan ₹69/mo par liya — 48 mahine ke liye ₹3,312 pay kiya, sab smooth. Phir renewal ka mail aata hai: **ab ₹289/mo**. Premium liya tha ₹149/mo par? Renewal **₹449/mo**. Ye "price increase" nahi hai jo achanak hua ho — ye page par pehle se likha hota hai, bas hum padhte nahi.

Is article me humne 17 October 2026 ko hostinger.com/in kholkar har plan ka intro aur renewal price dobara verify kiya hai, aur chaar alternatives — MilesWeb, Bluehost India, BigRock (HostGator India ka successor) — ke real numbers compare kiye hain. Jahan number official page se confirm nahi hua, wahan saaf "unverified" likha hai.

**Honesty note:** Ye article official pricing pages ke screenshots aur public data ke analysis par based hai. Koi fake "maine personally test kiya" claim nahi hai — jo confirm nahi hua, wo guess karke nahi likha.

## Renewal shock se bachne ke 3 practical tareeke

**1. Sabse lamba term pehle hi lock karo.** Intro price poori term par lagta hai. 48-month term lene par 4 saal tak ₹149/mo milta hai; 12-month term loge to per-month rate bhi zyada hoga aur renewal jaldi aayega. 30-day money-back guarantee hai, isliye "pehle test kar lein" ke liye chhota term lene ka logic weak hai.

**2. Renewal se 30–45 din pehle migrate kar jao.** Free migration aksar hosts offer karte hain (MilesWeb free migration deta hai). Renewal date calendar me mark karo — auto-renewal on rakhkar so jana sabse mehenga tareeka hai.

**3. Predictable-renewal host choose karo.** Kuch hosts renewal structure saaf rakhte hain. Neeche alternatives me isi par detail hai.

Agar aap abhi plan choose karne ki stage par ho to [Single vs Premium ka detail comparison](/hostinger-single-vs-premium-plan/) bhi padh lo — kaunsa plan aapke use-case par fit baithta hai, ye renewal math se pehle ka sawal hai.
