## Demat Account Kya Hai? (2 Line Me)

Jab aap share kharidte ho, to wo share kagaz ki form me nahi aata — wo electronically aapke **demat account** me store hota hai. Bank account jaise paisa store karta hai, demat account waise hi shares, ETFs aur bonds store karta hai. Broker ke paas account kholne par demat + trading account dono milte hain, aur NSDL ya CDSL uske peeche ki depository hoti hai.

Ye article sirf jankari ke liye hai, financial advice nahi. Invest karne se pehle apna research karo.

## Hidden Charges: DP Charge aur Auto Square-Off

Brokerage aur AMC ke alawa kuch charges aise hain jo bill me baad me dikhte hain. Inhe "hidden" isliye kehte hain kyunki beginners inhe compare karna bhool jate hain.

**DP (Depository Participant) charge:** Jab aap share bechte ho (delivery sell), to depository ko ek charge jata hai.
- **Zerodha:** ₹15.34 per scrip (per ISIN) on sell — debit transaction par
- **Groww:** CDSL ke through ₹3.5 + ₹16.5 per debit transaction
- **Upstox:** ₹20 per scrip on sell
- **Angel One:** official pages se DP charge ka exact figure is comparison me verify nahi ho paya — account kholne se pehle broker se likhit me confirm kar lena

**Auto square-off charge:** Intraday position agar aap khud 3:15-3:20 PM tak band nahi karte, to broker auto square-off karta hai aur us par extra charge lagta hai. Zerodha is par ₹50 + GST leta hai (verified). Baaki brokers ke auto square-off charges unki charge list me check karna — intraday karne walo ke liye ye janna zaroori hai.

**Pledge/unpledge aur call-and-trade:** Margin ke liye shares pledge karne par bhi charge lagta hai (Zerodha par ₹30 + GST per request). Call par order lagane (call-and-trade) par bhi extra fee hoti hai. Beginners ko salah hai shuruaat me sirf app/website se order lagayein.

## Demat Account Kholne ke Liye Documents

Account kholna aaj-kal poori tarah online hai. Ye documents ready rakho:

1. **PAN card** — mandatory hai, bina PAN demat account nahi khulta
2. **Aadhaar card** — e-KYC aur e-sign ke liye (Aadhaar se linked mobile number chahiye OTP ke liye)
3. **Bank proof** — cancelled cheque ya bank statement (paise jama/nikalne ke liye)
4. **Income proof** — sirf F&O (derivatives) activate karne ke liye chahiye (salary slip, ITR, ya bank statement). Sirf delivery investing ke liye zaroori nahi
5. **Photo/signature** — webcam se live photo aur e-sign ho jata hai

Process simple hai: broker ki site/app par form bharo → Aadhaar OTP se e-KYC → documents upload → e-sign → 24-48 ghante me account active. Koi bhi step par paise nahi lagte — account opening in chaaro brokers par free hai.

## Beginners ke Liye 5 Practical Tips

1. **Pehle paper trading ya chhote amount se shuru karo.** ₹5,000-10,000 se shuruaat karke process samjho — order types (market, limit), holdings, settlement cycle (T+1).
2. **Nominee zaroor add karo.** Account kholte waqt ya baad me nominee add karna na bhoolo — ye aapke parivaar ke liye important hai.
3. **Dormant account ka dhyan rakho.** Agar account khol kar 1-2 saal use nahi kiya, to bhi AMC lagti rahegi. Use nahi karna to band karwana behtar hai.
4. **Ek hi broker par shuru karo.** Ek se zyada demat account legal hai, lekin beginner ke liye ek account manage karna easy rehta hai aur AMC bhi ek hi lagti hai.
5. **Paisa invest karne ke saath seekhna bhi jaari rakho.** Demat account sirf tool hai — asli kaam company samajhna hai. Hamare finance guides padhte raho, aur agar credit side samajhna hai to [students ke liye pehla credit card guide](/students-ke-liye-pehla-credit-card/) bhi dekho.
