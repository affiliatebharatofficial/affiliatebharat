## Chhote business ke liye hosting = tax decision bhi hai

Jab ek dukaan, clinic, salon ya consultancy apni website banata hai, to hosting choose karna sirf "kaunsa plan sasta hai" ka sawal nahi hota. Agar aapka business **GST-registered** hai, to hosting bill par laga 18% GST aap **input tax credit (ITC)** ke roop me wapas claim kar sakte ho — matlab wo 18% aapke liye effectively cost-neutral ho jata hai. Par sirf ek condition par: aapke paas **proper GST tax invoice** hona chahiye, jisme aapka **GSTIN** printed ho.

Galat vendor ya galat billing setup chun liya, to har saal 18% extra real cost ban jayegi — ek chhote business ke liye ye chhoti rakam nahi hai. Is guide me hum wahi batayenge jo verified public data se pata chalta hai: kaunsa host GST invoice properly deta hai, displayed prices me GST included hai ya nahi, aur ITC claim karne ka sahi process kya hai.

**Honesty note:** Ye article public pricing pages, official FAQ/help pages aur reputable third-party sources ke analysis par based hai — koi fake "maine khud test kiya" claim nahi. Jahan info official page se confirm nahi hui, wahan saaf likha hai.

## Pehle tax samajh lo: hosting par 18% GST kyun lagta hai

India me web hosting ek **service** hai, aur IT services par GST rate **18%** hai. September 2025 me GST slabs me bade changes hue (12% aur 28% ke brackets hataye gaye), par IT services ka 18% rate waisa hi raha.

Ek cheez jo har small business owner ko confuse karti hai: **displayed price vs checkout price**. Hamari verified research me ye pattern mila:

- **MilesWeb** apne official FAQ me saaf likhta hai: *"All MilesWeb hosting prices shown are exclusive of GST. An 18% GST is added at checkout."*
- **Hostinger India** ki displayed prices bhi third-party sources ke mutabiq GST exclude karti hain — 18% checkout par add hota hai (ye official pricing page se directly confirm nahi ho paya, isliye third-party info ke roop me lein).
- **Bluehost** ke official tax help page ke mutabiq India customers se 18% GST collect kiya jata hai.

Matlab: **plan choose karte time displayed monthly price ko 1.18 se multiply karke asli kharcha nikalo** — ya agar GST-registered ho to ITC claim ka plan banao (neeche detail me).

## GST invoice aur ITC: asal me kaam kaise karta hai

Concept simple hai, par execution me 4 conditions hain. Ek bhi miss hui to credit nahi milega:

1. **GST-registered business:** Aapke paas valid GSTIN hona chahiye.
2. **Composition scheme me nahi:** Composition wale businesses ITC claim nahi kar sakte (neeche FAQ me detail).
3. **Business use:** Hosting aapke business ke kaam ke liye honi chahiye — personal blog ke liye li hui hosting par ITC nahi milega.
4. **Proper tax invoice:** Invoice par aapka GSTIN printed hona chahiye, aur wo invoice aapke **GSTR-2B** me reflect honi chahiye. Ye tabhi hota hai jab vendor time par apna **GSTR-1** file karta hai.

Ek example se samjho: aapne ₹10,000 (GST se pehle) ki hosting li. Checkout par 18% GST = ₹1,800 add hua, total bill ₹11,800. Agar upar ki chaaron conditions poori hain, to wo ₹1,800 aap apne output tax se adjust kar sakte ho — matlab hosting ka **effective cost ₹10,000** hi raha. Yehi wajah hai ki GST-registered businesses ke liye "sasta plan" ka matlab non-registered businesses se alag hota hai.

**Sabse important practical tip:** Hosting account banate hi billing settings me apna GSTIN daal do. Purane invoices par baad me GSTIN add karwana bahut painful hota hai aur aksar possible hi nahi hota. Pehla invoice aate hi check karo ki usme aapka GSTIN printed hai.

## Red flags: invoice ya checkout me ye dikhe to savdhaan

- **GST charge hua, par invoice par GSTIN nahi printed:** Aisa invoice ITC ke liye bekaar hai. Vendor se corrected tax invoice maango, aur na mile to use vendor avoid karo.
- **Quote me "all inclusive" bola, checkout par 18% add ho gaya:** Ye common trick hai. Quote stage par hi likhit me confirm karo ki price GST-inclusive hai ya exclusive.
- **Cash discount, bill nahi:** Kuch chhote vendors "bill ke bina sasta" offer karte hain. Isme aap **teen cheezen khote ho** — ITC, expense deduction (income tax me kharcha show nahi kar sakte), aur domain/hosting ke ownership ka clean proof. Business asset ke liye ye deal kabhi worth nahi hai.

## GST-compliant hosting lene ka 5-step checklist

1. **Signup se pehle GSTIN ready rakho.** Account banate hi billing/tax settings me daal do — "baad me kar lenge" sabse common aur mehengi galti hai.
2. **Checkout page par GST breakup verify karo.** CGST 9% + SGST 9% (ya IGST 18%) clearly show hona chahiye. Sirf ek lump-sum total dikhe to screenshot lo aur support se tax invoice confirm karo.
3. **Pehla invoice aate hi GSTIN check karo.** Invoice par aapka GSTIN printed hona chahiye. Nahi hai to turant support ko likho — pehle billing cycle me correction sabse easy hota hai.
4. **Har invoice save karo + CA se ITC confirm karwao.** Invoice aapke GSTR-2B me reflect ho raha hai ya nahi, ye aapke CA/accountant se verify karwao — khaas taur par pehli baar.
5. **Renewal price likh kar rakho.** Calendar me renewal date ka reminder lagao. Hostinger jaise hosts par renewal 3x hota hai — us time dusra host evaluate karna [hostinger single vs premium plan](/hostinger-single-vs-premium-plan/) jaisi guides se easy hota hai.
