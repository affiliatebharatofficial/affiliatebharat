## 3. Demat/Brokerage Charges (official pages, 2026-10-09)

### Zerodha — OFFICIAL PAGE (zerodha.com/charges, fetched 2026-10-09)
- Equity delivery: **₹0 (zero brokerage)**
- Equity intraday: **0.03% or ₹20 per executed order, whichever is lower**
- F&O futures: **0.03% or ₹20/executed order, whichever is lower**; F&O options: **flat ₹20 per executed order**
- Account opening: individual **free**; minor free; NRI ₹500; HUF free (online) / ₹500 (offline); partnership/LLP/corporate ₹500 (offline)
- Demat AMC: **free for the first year** on all new resident individual accounts; non-BSDA: ₹300/yr + 18% GST (charged quarterly); BSDA: free up to ₹4L holdings, ₹100/yr ₹4–10L, ₹300/yr above ₹10L
- DP charge: ₹15.34 per scrip on sell; call & trade: ₹50/order; direct mutual funds: free (₹0 commissions & DP)
Source: http://zerodha.com/charges/ (accessed 2026-10-09)

### Groww — OFFICIAL PAGE (groww.in/pricing, fetched 2026-10-09)
- Account opening: **₹0** (trading & demat)
- AMC: **₹0** (zero maintenance charges)
- Equity brokerage (delivery & intraday): **₹20 or 0.1% per executed order, whichever is lower, minimum ₹5**
- Mutual funds & IPOs: ₹0 brokerage
- Auto square-off: ₹50 per position; pledge ₹20 per ISIN
- DP charges (per sell transaction): CDSL ₹3.5 (male) / ₹3.25 (female) + Groww ₹16.5 (₹0 if debit value < ₹100)
- MTF: brokerage 0.1% per order; interest 14.95% p.a.
- F&O equity brokerage not visible in fetched page text — third-party (chittorgarh.com) reports ₹20 per executed order for F&O → mark UNVERIFIED on official page.
Source: https://groww.in/pricing (accessed 2026-10-09)

### Upstox — OFFICIAL PAGE (upstox.com/brokerage-charges, fetched 2026-10-09)
- Account opening: **Free**
- AMC: **₹0 for first year** (new customers); non-BSDA: **₹300 + GST** thereafter
- Equity delivery brokerage: **₹20 per executed order** (flat)
- Equity intraday: **₹20 per executed order or 0.1%, whichever is lower**
- Equity futures: **₹20 or 0.05%, whichever is lower**; equity options: **flat ₹20 per executed order**
- Mutual funds & IPOs: zero commission
- DP charges: ₹20 per scrip per day, sell only (CDSL ₹3.5 + Upstox ₹16.5; female: ₹3.25 + ₹16.75)
- Auto square-off ₹75 + GST; call & trade ₹75 + GST
- Upstox Plus plan: brokerage up to ₹30/order
Source: https://upstox.com/brokerage-charges/ (accessed 2026-10-09)

### Angel One — OFFICIAL PAGE (partial)
- Demat AMC: **free for 1st year; ₹240/year from 2nd year onward** (per official terms page: angelone.in/disclaimer)
- Account opening: **free** (per official open-demat-account FAQ)

## 4. SaaS Affiliate Programs (recurring commissions)

### GetResponse — VERIFIED, OFFICIAL (getresponse.com help + legal terms)
- **Recurring program: 33% default** (up to **40%** at 25+ sales/6 months, **50%** at 50+ sales/6 months)
- Bounty program: **$100 one-time** (Email Marketing products) / **$150** (Content Monetization products)
- Min payout **$50** ($200 for wire); paid around 20th of month
Sources: https://www.getresponse.com/help/when-will-i-get-paid.html ; https://www.getresponse.com/legal/affiliate-program (accessed 2026-10-09)

### SEMrush — THIRD-PARTY (reputable 2026 reviews; official page not opened)
- **$200 per new subscription sale (flat, NOT recurring)**; **$10 per free trial activation**
- 120-day cookie, managed via Impact, min payout ~$50, monthly payouts
- Important: the old BeRush 40% recurring structure is **discontinued** — current program is flat CPA (per flyingvgroup 2026 review referencing Semrush's affiliate page)
Sources: https://www.flyingvgroup.com/seotools/semrush-affiliate-program-review/ ; https://uppromote.com/affiliate-directory/semrush/ ; https://affgem.com/program/semrush/

### Kit (ConvertKit) — THIRD-PARTY (reputable; official page not opened)
- **30% recurring for 24 months** per referral (then 10% ongoing per quickads.ai); 60-day cookie
- Payments monthly via PayPal; min payout $100 per AFFMOJO (other sources say no minimum — conflicting)
Sources: https://saasaffiliate.com/partner/convertkit-affiliate-program/ ; https://www.quickads.ai/blog/beginners-guide-to-convertkit-affiliate-program ; https://www.affililist.com/affiliate/convertkit

### Systeme.io — VERIFIED, OFFICIAL (systeme.io/affiliate-program + official blog)
- **60% lifetime recurring commission** on every subscription sale; lifetime attribution (referral tagged permanently)

Sources: https://systeme.io/affiliate-program ; https://systeme.io/blog/successful-affiliate-marketing/ (accessed 2026-10-09)

### Jasper — THIRD-PARTY (PartnerStack terms listing + citations of official terms)

- 30-day cookie; min payout $5 (via PartnerStack)

Source: https://affiliatejob.org/programs/jasper (PartnerStack terms read 2026-09-02)

### ClickFunnels — THIRD-PARTY (community + affiliate directories; CF 2.0 era terms)
